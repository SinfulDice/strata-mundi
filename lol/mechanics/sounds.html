    NOIZEZ

    HOW IT WERKZ

    EVRY NOIZE IN TEH GAEM IZ MADE BY TEH GAEM ITSELF FROM RECIPEZ: FILTERD SHHH 4 SCUFFZ AN WINDY, TINY CRUNCHEZ 4 GRAVL, SNOW OR LEEFZ, RINGY BITZ 4 WUD, ROK, GLAS AN METUL, BUBBLEZ 4 WATUR AN HOT SOUP. EVRY NOIZE HAZ LOTZ OV FLAVURZ, AN EVRY TIEM IT GOEZ A BIT HIGHR OR LOWR: 2 STOMPZ NEVR SOUND TEH SAEM.

    WUT U HEERZ

    • STOMPZ: DEY SOUND LIEK TEH BLOK UNDR UR PAWZ (ROK, DIRT, GRAS, SAND, GRAVL, SNOW, WUD, GLAS AN ICE, LEEFZ, METUL) AN GO FASTR WEN U RUN.
    • BLOKZ GO BOOM AN PLOP: EVRY STUFF HAZ ITZ OWN; GLAS GOEZ CRASH.
    • WATUR: SPLOOSH WEN U FALL IN, SWISH WEN U SWIM.
    • HOT SOUP: TOP GOEZ BLUB; NEXT 2 A HOT SOUP LAEK U HEER BLUB LIEK EVRY SECKOND.
    • HOT FIRE: GOEZ CRACKL.

    NOIZEZ COME FROM WER DEY HAPPEN: QUIETR FAR AWAI, LEFT OR RITE DEPENDIN WER U LOOK, A BIT MUFFLD FROM BEHIND.

    WORLD NOIZE

    EVRY PLACE HAZ ITZ OWN BACKGROWND NOIZE, AN IT MIXEZ SLOWLY INTO TEH NEXT WEN U CHANGE BIOM:

    • GRASSY PLACEZ: LIL WINDY, BIT MOAR UP HIGH, WIF A WOOSH WUNCE IN A WHIEL.
    • TREEZ: LEEFZ GO SHHH.
    • JUNGL: LEEFZ AN BUGZ, LOUDR AT NITE.
    • SWAMP: AT NITE, BUGZ AN FROGGEHZ.
    • MOUNTINZ AN SNOW: WINDY, MOAR NEAR TEH TOPZ, AN SUM WOOSHEZ.
    • SEA, BIG SEA AN BEECHEZ: WAVEZ, U HEERZ DEM FROM TEH LAND NEAR BY.
    • DEZERT AN BADLANDZ: DRY WINDY AN FLYIN SANDZ.
    • UNDR TEH GROWND: DEEP RUMBL AN WATUR GO PLIP FAR AWAI.

    ECHOEZ AN WALLZ

    WIF FANCY SOUNDZ IN SPAEC, U CAN HEER TEH PLACE: NOTHIN OUTSIED, LIL ECHO IN A HOUSE, MOAR IN A SMOL CAVE, LONG RINGY IN A BIG CAVE. A NOIZE HIDIN BEHIND FULL BLOKZ COMEZ MUFFLD AN QUIETR; LEEFZ, GLAS AN WATUR MUFFL IT LES.

    MUZIK

    SUMTIEMZ A CALM SONG COMEZ: PIANO ON SOFT CHORDZ, WIF LOTZ OV QUIET TWEEN TEH BITZ. EVRY WORLD HAZ ITZ OWN THEEM; TERRA ONE ALSO PLAYZ AT TEH TITLE SCREEN. GAEM MAKEZ SUM SONGZ OV TEH THEEM, 1 TO 3 MINITS EACH, AN SAVEZ DEM TEH FURST TIEM SO IT NEVR MAKEZ DEM AGAIN. LOTZ OV MINITS OV QUIET TWEEN 2 SONGZ: MUZIK STAYZ A SURPRIZE.

    KNOBZ

    SOUNDZ TAB OV TEH KNOBZ: ALL TEH LOUD, DEN BLOKZ (STOMPZ, BOOM, PLOP, WATUR, HOT SOUP, FIRE), BAKGROUND NOIZEZ (WINDY, LEAFS, WAVEZ, BUGZ, DRIPZ) AN MUZIK, PRESETZ NEVR TUCH DEM, AN HOW OFTEN MUZIK (RAER, NORML OR LOTZ); MUZIK LOUDNES AT 0 TURNZ IT OFF. SOUNDZ IN SPAEC: FANCY (ECHOEZ AN MUFFLD NOIZEZ) OR SIMPL STEREO (ONLY FAR AN LEFT/RITE, LES THINKIN); DS AN POTATO PRESETZ GO SIMPL STEREO. NO SPEAKRZ? GAEM STILL WERKZ, SILENT.

    SOUNDZ WRITTED

    TURN DEM ON IN TEH SOUNDZ TAB: EVRY NOIZE GETZ WRITTED AT TEH BOTTOM RITE, WIF ARROW TO IT (← LEFT, → RITE, ↑ IN FRONT, ↓ BEHIND). A LINE GOEZ AWAI 3 SECONDZ AFTR TEH LAST NOIZE; UR OWN STOMPZ R NOT WRITTED.

    LOOKZ PAKZ

    A LOOKZ PAK CAN SWAP A NOIZE 4 A RECORDIN: A sounds/<name>.ogg FILE TAKEZ TEH PLACE OV TEH RECIPE WIF TEH SAEM NAEM, AN music/<theme>/*.ogg FILEZ SWAP TEH MADE SONGZ OV A THEEM.
