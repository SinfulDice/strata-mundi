    HOW TEH WORLD IZ MADE

    HOW IT WORKZ

    TEH WURLD IZ NOT DRAWED BY PAW: IT IZ CALCULATED FROM A SEED, BIT BY BIT WHILE U WALK. SAEM SEED, SAEM WURLD, BLOK BY BLOK, IN ANY ORDR. IT IZ KINDA INFINIT: ONLY TEH WURLD EDGE U PIKD STOPZ IT.

    GROUND AN BIOMZ

    4 INVIZIBUL MAPZ COVR TEH WURLD: HOT OR COLD, WET OR DRY, HOW FAR FROM TEH SEA AN HOW WORN DOWN (FROM OLD FLAT LAND 2 YUNG LAND). TEH LAST 2 GIVE TEH GROUND HEIGHT: DEEP BIG WATUR, SOFT HILLZ, POINTY BIG ROCKZ. TEH GROUND IZ 3D NAO: CLIFFZ, ROCK ROOFZ AN ROCK BRIDJEZ WER TEH LAND IZ YUNG, STAIRZ HILLZ FAR FROM TEH SEA, AN WIGGLY WATUR DAT GOEZ AWAY UP HIGH AN LEAVEZ DRY VALLYZ. TEH BIOM, PIKD FROM TEH WEATHR KIND, ONLY DECIDEZ WUT IZ ON TOP AN WUT GROWZ, AN GOIN FROM ONE 2 ANOTHR IZ SOFT. BIOMZ R HUNDREDZ OV BLOKZ BIG AN FOLLO HOT STRIPEZ: SANDY HOT PLAEC NEVR TOUCHEZ TEH SNOW, DER IZ ALWAYZ GRAS OR TREEZ IN BETWEEN. 19 BIOMZ 4 NAO.

    UNDR TEH GROUND

    UNDR TEH BIOM DIRTZ COMEZ ROCK, DEEP ROCK, DEN BOTTUM ROCK. WATUR FILLZ TEH LOW PLAECEZ UP 2 WATUR LINE. WIGGLY DARK HOLEZ GO THRU TEH ROCK BUT NEVR OPEN UNDR TEH SEA, IN TEH ROCK STUFFZ MAEK LIL STRIPEZ DEPENDIN ON DEEPNESS, RARE BIG ROCK STASHEZ OV IRON OR COPPR R LOTZ OV BLOKZ LONG (A FEW COLOR ROCKZ ON TEH GROUND TELL WER), AN TREEZ AN POKEY PLANTZ GROW DEPENDIN ON TEH BIOM.

    KINDZ OV WURLD

    WEN U MAEK WURLD, KIND PICKZ IT: NORMUL, FLAT LIEK PANCAEK (LAYRZ OV BLOKZ), SUPER BUMPY (WAY TALLR MOUNTINZ), WUN BIOME ONLY (U PICK WICH) OR LOTZ OF ILANDZ (MOSTLY WATUR WIF ILANDZ). TWEEK TEH WURLD… OPENZ TEH MAKER: BIOME SIZE, WATR LEVL, HOW MANY HOLEZ AN SHINIEZ (100%: NORMUL WURLD); FOR SUM KINDZ, TEH LAYRZ OV TEH PANCAEK (MOAR, LES, UP, DOWN, WICH BLOK, HOW THIK) OR TEH BIOME. A TINY MAP SHOWZ WAT U GET RITE AWAY, 2 KM WIDE (MAP PEEK OPSHUN: SHARP, BLURRY OR NO PEEK).

    TEH CODE TAB GIVEZ SHORT TEXT WIF ALL TEH KNOBZ (S1-…): COPEE IT FOR FRENZ, PASTE THEIRZ TO GET SAEM KIND OV WURLD. BROKED CODEZ OR CODEZ FROM TEH FUCHUR R SAYD NO. TEH SEED IZ SEPRIT: SAEM CODE AN SAEM SEED, SAEM WURLD.

    WER U START

    FLAT GRAS OR LOTZ OF TREEZ, ON DRY GROUND, WIF WATUR CLOSE.

    HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

    EXPLOR! F3 SHOWZ WER U R AN HOW HIGH, /seed SHOWZ TEH SEED. ALL DIS IZ WRITTEN IN TEH BASIC PAK, AN OTHR PAKZ CAN CHANGE IT.
