    WET STUFFZ

    WUT IZ DIS

    WATUR AN HOT JUICE FLOWZ. EVRY WET STUFF HAZ SAUCEZ (TEH WATUR OV SEAZ AN LAEKZ, TEH HOT JUICE LAEKZ WAI DOWN) AN FLOWZ DAT GET MOAR AN MOAR THIN: DOWN FURST, DEN 2 TEH SIDEZ, 2 TEH CLOSEST SLOEP. IF WATUR SEEZ HOEL 4 BLOKZ AWAI OR LES, IT GOEZ ONLY DAT WAI: BROKEN LAEK = LIL STREEM GOIN DOWN TEH STAIRZ, NOT BIG FLOOD. NO HOEL? IT GETZ WIDE IN ALL 4 WAIZ. AT TEH BOTTOM OV FALL, WATUR IZ FULL AGAIN AN GOEZ AGAIN. 2 SAUCEZ NEXT 2 EACH OTHR ON GROUND OR SAUCE = 3RD SAUCE. WATUR GOEZ UP 2 7 BLOKZ ON FLAT GROUND, HOT JUICE ONLY 3, AN WAI SLOWR. TAEK AWAY TEH SAUCE AN TEH FLOW GOEZ AWAY. WATUR + HOT JUICE = ROCK OR SHINY DARK ROCK.

    WUT U SEEZ

    TEH TOP OV WET STUFF LEANZ WIF TEH FLOW: FULL NEAR TEH SAUCE, ALMOST FLAT AT TEH END. 2 BLOKZ OV TEH SAEM WET STUFF NEVR HAZ A WALL BETWEEN, AN WET STUFF WIF MOAR OV ITSELF ON TOP FILLZ ALL ITZ BLOK (WATURFALLZ). WATUR IZ SEE-THRU, HOT JUICE NOT; TEH TOP WIGGLEZ, AN TEH GRAFIX OPSHUN "WIGGLY WATUR" STOPZ DAT.

    SWIMMIN

    • HOLD JUMP: U GO UP, TIL U FLOAT AT TEH TOP.
    • U GO HALF AS FAST IN WATUR, EVEN SLOWR IN HOT JUICE.

    • HEAD IN WET STUFF = ALL FOG, BLU (WATUR) OR ORANJ (HOT JUICE).
