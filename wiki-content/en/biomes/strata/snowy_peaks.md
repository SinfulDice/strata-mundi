## Description

The top of the highest mountains: everlasting snow on ledges and slopes alike, bare rock on the cliff walls, and a breathtaking view.

## Obtaining

Snowy peaks cap the mountains above about 230 blocks high, whatever the climate below.

## Usage

Snow blocks, stone, and the best viewpoint in the world.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: **snow** on ledges and steep slopes (two blocks thick on ledges), stone on the cliff walls.
- **Water**: frozen on top.
- **Vegetation**: none.

## Tips

Climb up a gentle slope rather than a cliff: the snow covers the places where you can set foot.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: the snow on top is enough to recognise the peaks. F3 shows your altitude.
