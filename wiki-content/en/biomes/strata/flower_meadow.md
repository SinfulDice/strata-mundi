## Description

A meadow covered in flowers of every colour, poppies, dandelions, cornflowers, daisies and lavender, with a few lone trees. A real open-air bouquet.

## Obtaining

Flower meadows appear in temperate regions, between the plains and the forests. It's one of the biomes where the world can make you **start**.

## Usage

Flowers galore for decorating, and open ground that's ideal for building.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: grass on dirt, sand under the water.
- **Vegetation**: loads of flowers, tall grass, a few bushes, rare oaks and birches.

## Tips

Pick one of each colour: the meadow has five kinds.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: the carpet of flowers is enough to recognise the meadow.
