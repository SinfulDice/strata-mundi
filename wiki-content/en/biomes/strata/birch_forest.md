## Description

A bright forest of white-trunked birches, sprinkled with flowers and ferns. You can wander here without ever feeling lost.

## Obtaining

Birch forests appear in cool, fairly humid regions. Like the plains, the forest and the flower meadow, it's a biome where the world can make you **start**.

## Usage

Pale birch wood, flowers to pick and a peaceful setting for a first house.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: grass on dirt, sand under the water.
- **Vegetation**: packed birches, a few oaks, tall grass, flowers, ferns.

## Tips

The white trunks can be seen from afar: perfect for finding your way.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: the white trunks are enough to recognise the birch forest.
