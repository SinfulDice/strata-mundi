## Description

Dizzying ridges, deep valleys and snowy peaks scratching the sky. The mountains are the biome of breathtaking views and big, slightly mad projects.

## Obtaining

Mountains rise inland, where the terrain is young and barely eroded. They form sharp ridges separated by wide valleys, and they're by far the highest terrain in the world.

## Usage

Lookouts, clifftop castles, tunnels through the rock, and stone cliffs where ores sometimes show.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: grass blocks on the slopes, with dirt underneath.
- **Altitude**: above a certain height, grass gives way to bare stone, then, higher still, to caps of snow blocks.
- **Vegetation**: firs on the grassy slopes.
- **Shores**: gravel along the water.

## Tips

Fly to the summit and look around: it's the best lookout to spot neighbouring biomes. F3 tells you how high you've climbed.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet, but mountains can be seen from very far away. Climb up and watch the blocks change with altitude.
