## Description

The great depths, far from the coasts: dark water over a gravel floor, sometimes more than forty blocks below the surface.

## Obtaining

Deep oceans fill the open sea, where the continent is furthest away.

## Usage

Gravel, lots of water, and the joy of feeling very small.

## Behaviour

- **Floor**: gravel.
- **Water**: static, very deep.
- **Vegetation**: none yet.

## Tips

The darker blue of the water marks the great depths: on the world creation map too.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: F3 shows your altitude, and the floor is far below the surface.
