## Description

A cold forest of tightly packed firs, its ground covered in brown podzol and needles, where ferns and mushrooms grow.

## Obtaining

Taigas appear in cold, humid regions, between the tundra and the birch forests.

## Usage

Plenty of fir wood, podzol, mushrooms and a mountain-cabin mood.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: **podzol** on dirt, gravel under the water.
- **Vegetation**: many firs, ferns, tall grass, small mushrooms.

## Tips

Mushrooms like to grow at the foot of firs: keep an eye on the ground as you walk.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: the brown ground and packed firs are enough to recognise the taiga.
