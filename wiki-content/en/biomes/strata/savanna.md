## Description

A hot, dry expanse covered in tall grass, dotted with acacias with leaning trunks and flat crowns.

## Obtaining

Savannas appear in hot, rather dry regions, between deserts and forests.

## Usage

Orange acacia wood, wide open spaces and a stunning view to build in.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: grass on dirt, sand under the water.
- **Vegetation**: scattered acacias, lots of tall grass, a few bushes.

## Tips

Acacias stand out from afar thanks to their flat crowns: a great landmark in the tall grass.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: the acacias are enough to recognise the savanna.
