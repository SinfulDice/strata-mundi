## Description

Water, water, and more water. The ocean stretches away from the continents, deep and blue, with here and there a sandy island poking its nose out.

## Obtaining

The ocean fills the regions farthest from inland, where the ground dives well below sea level.

## Usage

Long crossings, underwater bases, lighthouses on sandy islets, and a huge supply of gravel on the bottom.

## Behaviour

- **Water**: static, up to sea level. It doesn't flow and you pass through it like air.
- **Floor**: a layer of gravel, then stone.
- **Land above water**: sand, on top and underneath.
- **Vegetation**: none, for now.
- Caves never open onto the sea floor: they stay under a rock ceiling, nice and dry.

## Tips

Make the most of still water: place blocks where the water is, then break them, and you get perfectly dry underwater rooms.

## Discovery in game

The biome name isn't shown yet: you recognise the ocean by the water stretching to the horizon. Fly over it, or check the floor's height with F3 to estimate its depth.
