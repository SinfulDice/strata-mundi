## Description

A strip of soft sand between the sea and the land, ideal for a nap in the sun.

## Obtaining

Beaches line low coasts, where the land slopes gently down to the sea. Steep coasts have no beach.

## Usage

Sand galore and easy access to the water.

## Behaviour

- **Ground**: sand several blocks deep, sand under the shallow water.
- **Vegetation**: none.
- A beach only rises a few blocks above the sea.

## Tips

Follow the beach to go around an island without getting lost.

The biome name isn't shown yet: the strip of sand along the water is enough to recognise the beach.
