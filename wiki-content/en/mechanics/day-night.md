## Principle

On Terra, a day lasts **20 minutes**: about 10 minutes of daylight and 10 of night, with a short twilight at each sunrise and sunset. The **sun** rises in the east, crosses the sky tilting a little towards the south, then makes way for the **moon**. The share of the day and the light left at night come from the dimension's pack, so a pack can change them.

## What you see

- **The sky** goes from daytime blue to the orange of twilight, then to the dark blue of night. The fog and the horizon follow.
- **The sun and moon** are big, crisp pixels. The moon has **8 phases**, one per day, from full moon to new moon and back.
- **Stars** twinkle at night and fade near the horizon and at dawn.
- **Clouds** drift gently, always the same way, very high up: above the hills, below the great peaks. Their colour follows the time (white by day, pinkish at twilight, dark grey at night). They only move while time passes: when the game is paused, they stop.
- **Daylight** drops at night but never to zero: you can still see a little by the glow of the sky. Placed lights (torches, lanterns, glowstone) make the difference.

## Setting the time

- `/time` reads or sets the world's time; `/daycycle` stops or restarts its course. Both are typed in creative mode.
- When creating a world, the **Day and night** button picks between time that goes by or a frozen clock. The choice is saved with the world.
- The time is saved with the world. It never goes back: the phases of the moon keep counting the days.
- Time stands still while the game is paused.

## Settings

Two graphics settings are about the sky (Graphics tab):
- **Stars**: on or off. Almost no cost. Off in the "Very low" preset.
- **Clouds**: **off**, **simple** (one flat layer, very light) or **full** (with volume, a little more work for the graphics card). "Very low" turns them off, "Low" and "DS" keep them simple, the others use full ones.

Changes apply right away. The dimension's pack also sets the length of the day, the amount of clouds and their speed.

## Tips

- Night has fallen? In creative, `/time set day` saves you waiting for the morning.
- To build with the sun standing still: `/time set noon` then `/daycycle off`.
- A full-moon night is the brightest to look at; count eight days from one full moon to the next.

## Discovery in game

The sky is found simply by playing: sun, moon and clouds are there from the first day. The commands are explained by `/help time` and `/help daycycle`, and the settings by their help sentences in the options menu. [LORE À ÉCRIRE]: what the people of Terra say about the sun and the moon.
