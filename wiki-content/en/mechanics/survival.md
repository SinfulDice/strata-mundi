## The gauges

In survival, the player has **health** (20 points, 2 per heart; the ring of vitality adds some), **stamina**, **hunger** and **thirst**. Health, stamina and mana (later, with magic) sit at the top left; hunger and thirst above the hotbar. Under water the **breath** bar appears. Every bar can be moved in the HUD editor. There is no armour bar: protection shows in the inventory.

- **Hunger**: sprinting, jumping, swimming and mining tire you ("exhaustion"); exhaustion first eats **saturation** (a hidden reserve), then hunger. Well fed (hunger nearly full, and not thirsty), health comes back by itself; starving, it slowly goes, but hunger alone never kills.
- **Thirst**: it goes down with time and effort. At zero, health slowly goes.
- **Stamina**: sprinting, jumping and dashing spend it; it comes back quickly at rest. Out of breath, you cannot sprint until it has come back a little. Heavy armour lowers it. You cannot sprint when too hungry either.
- **Breath**: it goes down under water; at zero, you drown. The amulet of short breath adds some.

When creating the world, hunger, thirst and stamina can each be turned off, and the speed of hunger and thirst can be set (25 % to 200 %). In creative, nothing goes down and nothing hurts.

## Eating, drinking, healing

Eat, drink or apply care by **holding the right button** (right trigger on a gamepad) with the item in hand; you do not eat when full. A **bowl** of stew gives the bowl back, a bottle gives the empty bottle back.

- **Water**: a **glass bottle** or a **leather waterskin** fills with a right click on a water source. By the sea (oceans, beaches) the water is **salty**: drinking it makes you even thirstier; boil it in a furnace or a smoker. Sneaking with an empty hand, you can also take a sip straight from a source. A waterskin holds four sips.
- **Food**: berries (bushes cut without a sickle), apples (oak leaves cut without shears), baked apples and roasted mushrooms (smoker or furnace), mushroom stew. Bread will come with crops, meat with animals.
- **Care**: the **bandage** and the **medical kit** stop bleeding and heal over a few seconds. One effect never stacks: a bandage and a stew together, only the stronger healing counts.
- A **backpack with the feeding upgrade** feeds you from what it holds by itself, only when nothing would be wasted.

## Damage

Falls (beyond three blocks), drowning, lava, fire (and burning that goes on until you dive in water), the head inside a block, hunger, thirst, bleeding. Armour lessens blows and falls, not hunger nor lava; resistances (like fire resistance) come from equipment, and later from potions and spells. After a hit, you are briefly safe from a weaker one. The screen flashes red and the view shakes for a moment: both can be set in the options (Interface).

At zero health, for now, you **faint** and wake up at the starting point with all your things. Real death (dropped items, tomb) comes with combat.

## Finding out in game

The tooltips of food, drinks and care items tell what they give; those of empty bottles and waterskins tell where to fill them.
