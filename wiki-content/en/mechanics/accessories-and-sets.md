## Accessories

Next to the armour, the inventory has five fixed slots: **two rings**, **an amulet**, **a belt** and **a cloak**. Special boots go in the boots slot. Accessories are made at the **jeweller**:

- **Ring of Vitality**: more health (it will count once health exists).
- **Amulet of Short Breath**: a little longer under water (once drowning exists).
- **Vial Belt**: potion slots, for when potions come.
- **Wool Cloak**: keeps the cold out (once temperature exists).

## No stacking

**The same effect does not stack**: two rings of vitality count only once, and when two pieces give the same effect, only the strongest counts. Better wear two different rings.

## Sets

Wearing several matching pieces of one material (leather, cloth, iron and gold mail or plate, diamond scale or plate) gives a **set bonus** that grows with the pieces: 2, 3 or 4. The bonus reached replaces the one before. A piece's tooltip tells how many pieces of its set you wear (2/4…) and which bonus is active. A broken piece does not count.

## Movement from equipment

- **Leaping Boots**: a second jump in the air (press Jump again).
- **Runner's Belt**: a short dash where you are going, with the "Dash" key (F, D-pad down), then a cooldown.
- **Crampon Boots**: walking into a wall climbs it; sneaking holds on.

The world option "Movement from equipment" turns them all off.
