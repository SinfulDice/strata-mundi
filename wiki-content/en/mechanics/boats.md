## Principle

A **boat** floats on the surface of water and lava, and never sinks. It comes in **oak**, **fir**, **birch**, **jungle** and **acacia**: the hull takes the planks of its wood. In creative, they are in the **Transportation** tab of the inventory.

## Placing, getting in, getting off

- **Right click** with a boat on water (or on the ground): it is put down, facing where you look.
- **Right click** on a free boat: you sit in it. The camera follows the boat, and you can still look around.
- **Sneak** (rebindable key, gamepad included): you get off, onto the bank beside it if it is close enough, otherwise on top of the boat. A reminder stays at the bottom of the screen while you sit.
- In creative, a **left click** on a boat takes it away.

## Steering

- **Forward** and **back** paddle; going backwards is much slower.
- **Left** and **right** turn the boat.
- On water, the boat goes fast and glides a little before stopping.
- On dry land, it moves **slowly** and stops at once.
- On **ice**, it slides almost without friction and goes **very fast**.
- It stops against blocks, and a bank higher than the water holds it back.

## Saving

Boats are saved with the world, and you are back in your seat if you left while sitting in one.
