## Principle

In survival, every item has its place: **27 bag slots**, the 9-slot **hotbar** (the one at the bottom of the screen), **4 armour slots** and the **off hand**. Items stack (64 for most blocks, 1 for tools and boats); a tool that wears shows a durability bar. The server decides everything: a click sends an intent, the answer updates the inventory.

## Opening and handling

The Inventory key opens the screen (in creative, the creative inventory opens, with every item). In the screen:
- **left click**: take a stack, put it, or swap with the slot's; you can also **drag** from one slot to another;
- **right click**: take half a stack, or put a single item;
- **Shift + click**: send the stack to the other side (hotbar ↔ bag, chest ↔ inventory; armour goes to its slot);
- **double click**: gather every identical item onto the held stack;
- **keys 1 to 9** over a slot: swap it with that hotbar slot;
- **click outside the window** or the Throw key: throw the item to the ground.

With a gamepad: A takes or puts, X takes half, Y transfers, R3 gathers, L3 throws, L1 locks, B closes.

## Comfort

- **Sort** by type or by name tidies the bag, merging stacks.
- **Locking** a slot (middle click or L1) protects it from sorting, gathering and stashing.
- **Stash** puts the bag's stacks into nearby chests that already hold those items.
- The **search** of an open chest darkens everything that does not match.

## Items on the ground

The Throw key throws the held item (with Sprint: the whole stack). A block broken in survival leaves its item on the ground. Items on the ground spin, gather when identical and close, are **picked up automatically** when you walk over them and **disappear after 5 minutes** (a duration set by the pack). They are saved with the world.

## Chests and barrels

Right click opens a chest or a barrel (sneaking puts a block against it). Two chests side by side make a double chest; barrels stay single. A broken container drops its contents.

## Discovery in game

The help at the bottom of the inventory screen recalls every gesture, with your device's keys.
