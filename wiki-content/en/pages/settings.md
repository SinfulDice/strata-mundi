## Settings for every machine

Strata Mundi's golden rule: **everything can be turned on, off or adjusted**. Whether you play on a powerful PC, an old laptop or the DS handheld console, you can tailor the game to your machine, your eyes and your habits. Each option comes with a short sentence explaining what it does, performance included.

## Presets

A preset sets all the graphics at once: **Very low**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Ultra**, and **DS**, designed for the small home-made handheld console. Touching a single graphics setting afterwards switches to **Custom**, without losing anything else.

## Applied right away

Changes apply **live**, without restarting: the game only redoes what changed (re-meshing the world, textures, window…). Settings are sorted into six tabs (Graphics, Interface, Controls, Keys, Game, Packs) and saved in a single file; an invalid value simply goes back to its default.

## Accessibility

A readable font, interface size, colour-blind filters, reduced camera effects and an interface layout editor are all in the Interface tab.
