## Welcome

Welcome to the **Strata Mundi** wiki, "the layers of the world"! It's a block-based building and exploration game: an endless world, calculated from a seed, for you to roam, dig and reshape as you please. For now (v0.1), you play in **creative mode**: every block is within reach, you can fly, and you build without limits.

## Finding your way around the wiki

- **Blocks**: one page per block, with its infobox (identifier, category, properties), where to find it and how to use it.
- **Biomes**: the world's landscapes, their ground and their vegetation.
- **Commands**: everything the console can do.
- **Mechanics**: world generation, saving, game modes.
- **Controls** and **Settings**: keys, gamepad and every option.

The search box at the top of the page finds any entry by its name. The language button switches between French, English and LOLcat, and dark mode follows your system (or its own button).

## The game stands on its own

Everything you need to know can be discovered **in game**: the targeted block's name at the top of the screen, the creative inventory and its search, the F3 screen, `/help` in the console. This wiki goes deeper; it never replaces the joy of discovery.

Happy exploring!
