## Description

Sandstone cut into smooth bands, the colour of the desert.

## How to get it

At the stonecutter (one sandstone gives one), or four sandstones in a square (four). In creative: Building tab.

## Uses

A full building block, mined with a pickaxe.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall.

## Tips

It dresses a desert house nicely.

## Discovery in game

The stonecutter offers it as soon as sandstone is laid in it.
