## Description

Stone bricks carved with a framed square, for doorsteps and pillars.

## How to get it

Right click with a chisel on stone bricks.

## Uses

A full building block, mined with a pickaxe and kept as it is.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall. Another stroke of the chisel moves on to the next pattern: patterned stone bricks.

## Tips

A row of chiseled bricks in the middle of a brick wall breaks the monotony.

## Discovery in game

The chisel's tooltip says that a right click works the aimed block.
