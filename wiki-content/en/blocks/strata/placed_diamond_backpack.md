## Description

A diamond-buckled backpack put down on the ground: 81 slots and 4 upgrade slots. Its buckles tell its tier.

## How to get it

A diamond backpack kit used (right click) on a gold-buckled backpack put down raises it **in place**, contents and upgrades kept. No recipe ever uses up a backpack: nothing is lost.

## Uses

Right click opens it like a chest. Its upgrades go in the panel on the right: the chests' (sorting, filter, compression, destruction, magnet) and its own (pickup, feeding, built-in crafting, tank).

## Behaviour

Broken, it becomes the backpack again **with all it holds**: nothing scatters. It does not burn. It takes no other bag. Its contents are saved with the world.

## Tips

Put it down near a building site as a makeshift chest, then take it all back at once.

## Discovery in game

The backpack's tooltip says how to open and put it down; the recipe book shows the backpack and the kits.
