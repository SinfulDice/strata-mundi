## Description

Bushy, bright green oak foliage, full of little gaps through which you can glimpse the sky. Perfect for a nap in the shade (symbolic shade, until light arrives).

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give oak_leaves`. In the world, they form the rounded crown of oaks, in plains and forests.

## Usage

Hedges, bushes, green roofs, garden mazes, camouflaged tree houses.

## Behaviour

A solid but **see-through** block: you can see through the gaps in the foliage, neighbouring leaves included. For now, leaves **don't fall** when you cut the trunk: a tree without its log proudly keeps its crown floating in mid-air. You can even walk on them.

## Tips

Lay a ring of oak-leaf hedge and you've got a secret garden, perfect for hiding your builds.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a tree crown to read its name at the top of the screen, or search "leaves" in the creative inventory.
