## Description

A stone hearth with a bed of embers in an iron rim: this is where ingots become mail rings, armour plates and mobility boots, and where diamonds are cut into scales.

## Obtaining

Three iron ingots above a furnace, surrounded by five cobblestones, at the crafting table. It is broken with a pickaxe. In creative: Workshops tab.

## Usage

Right click to open it. On the left, the list of everything the forge can make ("<" ">" arrows or the wheel when it overflows); on the right, the hearth and its four cells, then the result. Choose an item: its ingredients go from the inventory to the cells; take the result (shift-click: as many as possible).

## Behaviour

Each metal piece **burns one coal** (or charcoal), counted as an ingredient. Diamond scales are cut without fire. Closing the screen gives the items laid back.

## Tips

Two ingots and one coal give four rings: forge rings in batches before mail.

## Finding out in game

The forge's list and tooltips tell everything: ingredients, coal included, and what you lack.
