## Description

A fir trunk, darker and slimmer than an oak's, with deeply furrowed bark. It has spent its whole life in the cold and it shows: this log has character.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give fir_log`. In the world, it forms the trunks of firs, which grow on mountain slopes and, more sparsely, in the snowy tundra. They take root on grass blocks, snowy or not.

## Usage

Chalets, mountain huts, dark beams that contrast with pale planks. Laid on its side, it makes lovely railings.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block. Like every log, it is **oriented**: upright when placed on the top or bottom of a block, lying along the axis of the clicked face when placed against a side. The lying variants keep the same name and have no item of their own.

## Tips

Firs are taller than oaks: a middle click on the trunk is quicker than hunting for the log in the inventory.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a trunk to read its name at the top of the screen; F3 shows the exact identifier, lying variant included.
