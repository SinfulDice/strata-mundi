## Description

A massive iron block, its horn and its foot: this is where tools, weapons and armour are upgraded and repaired.

## Obtaining

Seven iron ingots at the crafting table (three, one in the middle, three). It is broken with a pickaxe. In creative: Workshops tab.

## Usage

Right click to open it. Lay the item in the left cell and what pays in the right one.

- **Upgrade**: choose an upgrade in the list (each is shown by its material; hover it to see its level, effects and cost), then take the result.
- **Repair**: with no upgrade chosen ("Repair" button), the item's material (an ingot of its tier, planks, stone, leather, cloth) gives back a quarter of its uses each; a second like item without upgrades adds its uses and 12 % more. A broken item kept (the "Worn-out items" option) is repaired too.

## Behaviour

Each repair of the same item costs one more experience point (up to 10). Experience costs are shown but free until experience exists. Closing the screen gives the items laid back. The anvil does not wear out.

## Tips

Iron has a single craft slot, gold and diamond two: choose well. Reinforcement and lightening do not go together.

## Finding out in game

The journal's recipe book shows how to make it once the recipe is learnt; the anvil's list shows everything an item can take.
