## Description

A small brown mushroom with a flat cap, discreet and a little shy, growing at the foot of trees.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Plants tab of the inventory, or `/give brown_mushroom`. In the world, it is found in small groups in **forests** (more common than the red mushroom).

## Usage

Decorating gardens, meadows and path edges; a touch of life around your builds.

## Behaviour

A **crossed** plant: two crossing planes, visible from every side. It has **no collision** (you walk through it) and hides no neighbouring block. You can aim at it and break it in one hit; it does not grow or spread after the world is generated.

## Tips

Since it blocks nothing, you can scatter some around a house without getting in the way.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or open the Plants tab of the creative inventory.
