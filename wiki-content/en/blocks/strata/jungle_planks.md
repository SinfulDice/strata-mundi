## Description

Neatly lined-up jungle planks of warm and pinkish wood.

## Obtaining

In survival: one log gives four planks, in any crafting grid (a recipe known from the start). In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or `/give jungle_planks`.

## Usage

Floors, walls, decks and timber frames; their colour sets them apart from oak and fir planks.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block.

## Tips

A floor alternating two woods instantly makes a room look tidy.

## Discovery in game

Search for "planks" in the creative inventory.
