## Description

Dark brown fir planks with tight grain, smelling sweetly of resin and mountain chalets. Darker than oak planks, they give character to any room.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or `/give fir_planks`. They don't appear in nature; the creative inventory is their only source until crafting arrives.

## Usage

Chalets, imaginary libraries, hulls of boats that never leave the dock, dark rafters. They go very well with stone and sandstone.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block. Like oak planks, they neither burn nor rot for now.

## Tips

A fir-plank roof on stone walls: the perfect mountain hut, to be built just below the snowy peaks.

## Discovery in game

Open the Building tab of the creative inventory, or search "fir" to gather the whole family: log, planks and needles.
