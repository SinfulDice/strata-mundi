## Description

Two crossed logs blazing in a ring of stones. A place to warm your hands while watching the sparks rise.

## Obtaining

At the crafting table: three logs at the bottom, a coal (or charcoal) in the middle between three sticks. The recipe is found by trying. In creative: Building tab, or `/give campfire`.

## Uses

Warming up at night or in the snow, drying off after a swim, lighting a camp.

## Behaviour

Gives the strongest light (**level 15**) and the strongest heat after lava: **+14 °C** up close, up to 6 blocks away. It is not solid and must stand on a block. Walking into it burns a little, without setting your clothes on fire.

## Tips

Build a roof over the campfire: the shelter keeps the warmth in at night.

## Finding out in game

On a night in the snow, the temperature tip suggests a fire: try the recipe at the crafting table.
