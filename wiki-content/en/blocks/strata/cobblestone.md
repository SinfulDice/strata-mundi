## Description

Rounded stones held in rough mortar: what is left of stone once a pickaxe has been through. Less smooth, just as sturdy.

## How to get it

Breaking stone with a pickaxe gives cobblestone. In creative: Building tab, or `/give cobblestone`.

## Uses

A sturdy, plentiful building block. It will soon build the first furnaces.

## Behaviour

Full, opaque block; it needs a pickaxe to be collected.

## Tips

A mine gallery quickly fills the inventory with cobblestone: keep a chest of it near the entrance.

## Discovery in game

Break stone with a pickaxe: it drops in this form.
