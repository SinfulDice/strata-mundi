## Description

A rust-coloured red-brown rock, as if the stone had spent centuries in the rain. It makes up the body of giant iron deposits.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give ferrous_rock`. In the world, it is found in giant iron deposits, rare and rich, stretching for dozens of blocks underground. A few blocks sometimes crop out on the surface, right above.

## Usage

A building block for now, with a warm, earthy tone.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, a little harder than stone.

## Tips

A patch of rust in a meadow is never an accident: an iron deposit sleeps somewhere underneath. Dig straight down.

## Discovery in game

Spot it while exploring: its colour stands out against grass, sand and grey stone.
