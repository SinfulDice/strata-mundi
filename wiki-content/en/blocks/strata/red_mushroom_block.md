## Description

A piece of a giant mushroom cap, bright red with white spots. It makes you want to sit underneath.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give red_mushroom_block`. In the world, it forms the **domed** cap of giant red mushrooms, in the giant mushroom lands.

## Usage

Round roofs, gnome houses, very eye-catching garden decorations.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, soft block: it breaks quickly.

## Tips

Put it on mushroom stems to build your own giant mushrooms.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a giant mushroom cap to read its name at the top of the screen.
