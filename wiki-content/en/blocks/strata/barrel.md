## Description

A barrel of iron-hooped staves: twenty-seven slots, like a chest, in a full block that stacks without fuss.

## How to get it

In creative: Storage tab of the inventory, or `/give barrel`. Crafting arrives later.

## Uses

Right click opens it; while sneaking, right click puts a block against it. Unlike the chest, **it never joins** its neighbours: whole walls of barrels stay as many separate stores.

## Behaviour

Its contents are saved with the world; broken, it drops them. It can burn, and its contents then fall to the ground.

## Tips

Line up barrels for a tidy cellar: each keeps its contents, and the screen's search quickly finds an item.

## Discovery in game

Open the Storage tab of the creative inventory, or type "barrel" in the search.
