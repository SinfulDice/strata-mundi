## Description

A sturdy oak top with a grid scored into it, a saw and a hammer hung on the side: where ideas become things.

## How to get it

Four planks, of any wood, in a square in the inventory's 2×2 grid. Known from the start. In creative: Building tab, or `/give crafting_table`.

## Uses

Right click opens a **3×3 grid**: every recipe too big for the inventory's grid (chest, barrel, boats…) is made there. The "Journal" button opens the recipe book, which lays the recipe for you.

## Behaviour

When the screen closes, what is left in the grid goes back into the inventory (or to the ground if it is full). Walking away or breaking the table closes the grid the same way.

## Tips

Laying ingredients at random is harmless: a valid combination you do not know yet still works and joins the recipe book. A small message tells when the items laid seem to go together.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book (Journal key, or the inventory's "Journal" button) shows it among the starting recipes.
