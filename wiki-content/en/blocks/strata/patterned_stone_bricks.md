## Description

Stone bricks adorned with a four-leaf rosette.

## How to get it

Right click with a chisel on chiseled stone bricks.

## Uses

A full building block, mined with a pickaxe and kept as it is.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall. Another stroke of the chisel brings back plain stone bricks.

## Tips

Alternated with chiseled bricks, they make a frieze.

## Discovery in game

By working chiseled bricks further with the chisel.
