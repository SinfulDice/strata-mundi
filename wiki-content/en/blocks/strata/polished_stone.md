## Description

Stone ground smooth, with a bevelled edge.

## How to get it

At the stonecutter (stone or stone bricks), or four stone bricks in a square (four polished stones). In creative: Building tab.

## Uses

A full building block; it goes into the blast furnace's recipe.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall.

## Tips

Just right for floors and doorsteps.

## Discovery in game

The stonecutter offers it as soon as stone is laid in it.
