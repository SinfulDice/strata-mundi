## Description

The water of seas, lakes and oceans, blue and translucent. It makes you want to dive in, will one day be home to fish, and always looks great on a postcard.

## Obtaining

Water **has no item**: you won't find it in the creative inventory, nor with `/give`. In the world, it fills every dip in the terrain up to sea level, from the vast ocean to the small lakes of the plains. In the snowy tundra, its surface is frozen.

## Usage

Scenery, natural border, landmark near the starting point.

## Behaviour

Water is **static**: it neither flows nor spreads. It isn't solid, so you pass through it like air (no swimming yet). It can't be targeted or broken; however, you can **place a block in its place**. If you break that block afterwards, it leaves a pocket of air: the water doesn't come back.

## Tips

Make the most of still water to build under the sea: place walls, break the inside, and there you are in a dry air bubble.

## Discovery in game

Since it can't be targeted, its name never appears at the top of the screen: you recognise it on sight, and a new world always starts you not far from it.
