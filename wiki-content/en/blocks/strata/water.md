## Description

The water of seas, lakes and oceans, blue and translucent. It makes you want to dive in, will one day be home to fish, and always looks great on a postcard.

## Obtaining

Water **has no item** of its own: in creative, the **water bucket** (Tools tab) pours a source with a right click, and the empty **bucket** takes it back. In the world, it fills every dip in the terrain up to sea level, from the vast ocean to the small lakes of the plains. In the snowy tundra, its surface is frozen.

## Usage

Scenery, natural border, landmark near the starting point.

## Behaviour

Water **flows**: a **source** (the water of seas and lakes) runs down first, then sideways up to **7 blocks**, heading for the nearest slope and getting thinner. The world's water stays still until disturbed: breaking the wall of a lake makes it flow. A new source forms between two sources standing on the ground. Removing the source makes the flow go away. Water washes plants away and turns lava into **stone** or **obsidian**. You **swim** in it: hold jump to rise, otherwise you sink slowly, and you move half as fast; against a bank, a little kick helps you climb out. With your head underwater, the view turns blue. Only an empty bucket can target it and it can't be broken, but you can **place a block in its place**. Its surface slopes with the flow and ripples gently (graphics option "Liquid animation"). The flow can be turned off with the world option `fluids` (`flow: false`).

## Tips

Make the most of still water to build under the sea: place walls, break the inside, and there you are in a dry air bubble.

## Discovery in game

Since it can't be targeted, its name never appears at the top of the screen: you recognise it on sight, and a new world always starts you not far from it.
