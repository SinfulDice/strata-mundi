## Description

A wooden frame with a stretched hide drying on it, next to a soaking tub: leather is prepared and leather armour sewn here.

## Obtaining

Sticks in a frame around a mud, on three planks of any wood, at the crafting table (known from the start). It is broken by hand or, faster, with an axe. In creative: Workshops tab.

## Usage

Right click to open it. At the top, the four cells in a row like hides on a rack, then the result; below, the list of what the tannery can make. Choose an item: its ingredients go from the inventory to the cells; take the result.

## Behaviour

For now, leather is made of two plant fibres and a mud: hides will come with animals. Closing the screen gives the items laid back.

## Tips

Leather is also used at the jeweller (belts) and the forge (mobility boots, from leather boots).

## Finding out in game

The tannery's list and tooltips tell everything: ingredients and what you lack.
