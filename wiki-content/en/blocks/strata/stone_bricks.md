## Description

Stone cut into even blocks laid in staggered courses: the classic of sturdy walls.

## How to get it

At the stonecutter (one stone gives one brick), or four stones in a square in the grid (four bricks). In creative: Building tab.

## Uses

A full building block, mined with a pickaxe.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall.

## Tips

Mixed with polished stone, it makes fine frames.

## Discovery in game

The stonecutter offers it as soon as stone is laid in it.
