## Description

The grass block is the world's green carpet: neatly mown grass on top, a green fringe spilling over the sides, and dirt underneath. It makes you want to have a picnic.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give grass_block`. In the world, it covers the ground of plains, forests and mountains, until the stone and snow of the peaks take over. By the water's edge, it gives way to shoreline sand or gravel.

## Usage

Gardens, lawns, green roofs. It's also where oaks and firs take root when the world is generated.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block, with different textures on top, sides and bottom. For now, it doesn't spread onto nearby dirt and doesn't wither under a block: it stays exactly as you placed it.

## Tips

For a flawless lawn on bumpy ground, level things out with dirt first, then add a single layer of grass on top.

## Discovery in game

It's often the very first block you see when you arrive. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or middle-click to add it to your hotbar.
