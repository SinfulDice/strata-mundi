## Description

Stone dotted with small coppery orange flecks that catch the torchlight. Copper is iron's warm cousin: showier, and almost as common.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give copper_ore`. In the world, it forms small veins in stone, halfway down under plains and hills. It also comes in bulk in the giant deposits of verdigris rock. In deep stone, it becomes deep copper ore.

## Usage

A decorative block for now: ores **can't be harvested yet**, and breaking one in creative simply makes it vanish.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as stone.

## Tips

A few green stones in the grass? Dig underneath: a giant copper deposit is probably close.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. F3 shows the altitude where you found it.
