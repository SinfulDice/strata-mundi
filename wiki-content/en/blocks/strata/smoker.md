## Description

A smoker: it only cooks **food**, twice as fast as a furnace (food comes with the survival gauges). Its front turns toward you when you place it and glows while the fire burns.

## How to get it

A furnace with four logs around it in a cross. Mine it with a pickaxe. In creative: Workstations tab.

## Uses

Right click: the item to cook on top, the fuel below (coal, charcoal, wood, lava bucket…), the result on the right. One item takes **5 s** and one coal cooks **8**. Upgrade slots: **0**.

## Behaviour

The fire only starts when there is something to cook and room in the output; a full output stops everything. It keeps cooking with its screen closed, as long as its area is loaded. Broken, it drops everything it holds.

## Tips

An iron furnace kit makes it faster and thriftier without emptying it. Shift-click puts each item in the right slot.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book in the journal shows how to make it once the recipe is learnt.
