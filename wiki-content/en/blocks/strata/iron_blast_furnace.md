## Description

A blast furnace of iron tier: it only smelts **ores**, twice as fast as a furnace. Its front turns toward you when you place it and glows while the fire burns.

## How to get it

In place, with a **iron furnace kit** used (right click) on a stone one; or at a crafting table, the stone one surrounded by eight ingots. Mine it with a pickaxe. In creative: Workstations tab.

## Uses

Right click: the item to cook on top, the fuel below (coal, charcoal, wood, lava bucket…), the result on the right. One item takes **4 s** and one coal cooks **10**. Upgrade slots: **1**.

## Behaviour

The fire only starts when there is something to cook and room in the output; a full output stops everything. It keeps cooking with its screen closed, as long as its area is loaded. Broken, it drops everything it holds.

## Tips

Modules (speed, fuel, double output) go in the upgrade slots; two identical modules do not add up. Shift-click puts each item in the right slot.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book in the journal shows how to make it once the recipe is learnt.
