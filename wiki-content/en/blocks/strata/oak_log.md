## Description

A handsome oak trunk with rough brown bark and clearly visible rings at each end. It smells of forests and childhood tree houses.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give oak_log`. In the world, it forms the trunks of oaks, scattered in the plains and packed tightly in forests. Oaks only grow on grass blocks.

## Usage

Beams, pillars, timber frames, window frames, rustic cabins. Laid on its side, it makes great lintels and garden benches.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block. The log is **oriented**: placed on the top or bottom of a block, it stands upright; placed against the side of a block, it lies down along the axis of the clicked face, bark and grain lined up. The lying variants have no item of their own: it's always the same log, with the same name.

## Tips

For a tidy timber frame, click the face pointing in the direction you want: that face decides the orientation.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a trunk: its name appears at the top of the screen. F3 also shows the exact identifier of the targeted block, lying variant included. A middle click on a tree gives you a copy.
