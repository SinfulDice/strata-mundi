## Description

A wooden bench with full drawers, a velvet cushion and a loupe: this is where rings, amulets, belts, cloaks and special boots are made.

## Obtaining

Two copper ingots, an iron ingot and three planks of any wood at the crafting table (copper, iron, copper above the planks). It is broken by hand or, faster, with an axe. In creative: Workshops tab.

## Usage

Right click to open it. The list shows everything the jeweller can make; hover an item to see its ingredients and whether you lack some. Choose it: its ingredients go from the inventory to the four cells, then take the result (shift-click: as many as possible).

## Behaviour

Ingredients can also be laid by hand, in any cell. Closing the screen gives the items laid back.

## Tips

Accessories are worn in their slots next to the armour (two rings, an amulet, a belt, a cloak); two like rings count only once.

## Finding out in game

The jeweller's list and the tooltips tell everything: ingredients, effects once worn.
