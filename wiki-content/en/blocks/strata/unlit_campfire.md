## Description

A campfire put out: charred logs on a bed of ash, in its ring of stones.

## Obtaining

Right-click a lit campfire with a shovel. Broken, it gives a campfire back.

## Uses

Keeping a fire ready without it burning or shining: handy for a hideout or a camp you leave.

## Behaviour

Gives no heat, no light and does not burn. Right-click it with flint and steel to light it again.

## Finding out in game

Try a shovel on a campfire: it goes out.
