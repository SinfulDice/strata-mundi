## Description

A gold-bound chest: 63 slots (7 rows of nine) and 2 upgrade slots. Its gold border tells its tier at a glance.

## How to get it

In creative: Storage tab of the inventory. In survival: eight ingots around a iron-bound chest, or **better**: a gold storage kit used (right click) on a iron-bound chest already placed, without emptying it.

## Uses

Right click opens it; the screen scrolls its rows (buttons of the right panel, mouse wheel). Its upgrades (sorting, filter, compression, destruction, magnet) go in the panel on the right. Two chests of the same tier side by side join into a double chest, and **the upgrades of both halves** work for the whole chest.

## Behaviour

Its contents and upgrades are saved with the world; broken, it drops everything, upgrades included.

## Tips

A magnet and a filter make a chest that gathers by itself what lies around the mine.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book shows the kit and the chest; an upgrade's tooltip says what it does.
