## Description

A cheerful jumble of little pebbles, grey, beige and brown. Gravel never quite managed to become stone, but it kept its good mood.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give gravel`. In the world, it carpets the ocean floor and forms the shores and shallow beds along forests, mountains and snowy tundra.

## Usage

Garden paths, mountain trails, the banks of an artificial river. It gives a natural, slightly rustic look to anything it touches.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block. Like sand, **it doesn't fall yet**: until gravity arrives, it stays perched wherever you put it, even over thin air.

## Tips

Head out to sea: the ocean floor is a huge open-air gravel quarry (well, open-water).

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. Underwater, the outline of the targeted block helps you tell gravel from sand.
