## Description

Brown earth covered in fir needles and dead leaves, smelling nicely of cold forest.

## Obtaining

In creative: Nature tab of the inventory, or `/give podzol`. In the world, it covers the ground of taigas.

## Usage

Undergrowth, forest trails, rustic gardens.

## Behaviour

Full, opaque block; the underside is dirt. Firs, ferns and mushrooms grow on it.

## Tips

Brown ground under packed firs: you're in a taiga.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
