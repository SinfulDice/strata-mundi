## Description

Oak planks lined with colourful books. Stacked, bookshelves **connect**: the borders disappear and they make one big piece of furniture.

## How to get it

In the creative inventory, Building tab.

## Behaviour

A full block with planks on top and bottom. Its sides change with the bookshelves above and below (connected texture). It is **flammable**: fire catches it quickly.

## Tips

The "Texture variants" graphics setting turns the connection off if you prefer separate blocks.

## Discovering it in game

Stack three bookshelves: the furniture is drawn in one piece.
