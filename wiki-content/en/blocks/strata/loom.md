## Description

A wooden frame strung with threads, the cloth growing at the bottom and two spools on the beam: cloth and wool are woven and clothes sewn here.

## Obtaining

Three sticks above three plant fibres, on three planks of any wood, at the crafting table (known from the start). It is broken by hand or, faster, with an axe. In creative: Workshops tab.

## Usage

Right click to open it. On the left, the four cells in a column like a warp; in the middle, the list of what the loom can make; on the right, the result. Choose an item: its ingredients go from the inventory to the cells; take the result.

## Behaviour

Three fibres make cloth; for now, four fibres make wool, until sheep come. Closing the screen gives the items laid back.

## Tips

The wool cloak, which keeps the cold out, is made here; cloth is also used at the jeweller (amulet).

## Finding out in game

The loom's list and tooltips tell everything: ingredients and what you lack.
