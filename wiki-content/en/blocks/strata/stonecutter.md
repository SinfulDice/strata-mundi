## Description

A round iron blade on a stone slab: it cuts a block into its variants, with no fuel.

## How to get it

An iron ingot over three stones, at a crafting table. Mine it with a pickaxe. In creative: Workstations tab.

## Uses

Right click: lay a block in the left cell, choose a variant in the list (stone → stone bricks or polished stone, sandstone → cut sandstone), then take the result. Shift-click cuts the whole stack.

## Behaviour

Each cut uses one block. Closing the screen gives the block laid back to the inventory.

## Tips

Simpler than the crafting table: one stone gives one brick, nothing lost. More block families (slabs, stairs…) will come.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book in the journal shows how to make it once the recipe is learnt.
