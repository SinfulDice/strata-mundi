## Description

Deep, dense green foliage, full of little holes the sky shines through.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give jungle_leaves`. In the world, they form the crown of jungle trees in **jungles**.

## Usage

Hedges, green roofs, gardens and tree houses in jungle colours.

## Behaviour

A solid but **see-through** block. For now, leaves do not fall when you cut the trunk.

## Tips

Their shade stands out from afar: handy to spot these trees from a hilltop.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a tree crown to read its name, or search for "leaves" in the creative inventory.
