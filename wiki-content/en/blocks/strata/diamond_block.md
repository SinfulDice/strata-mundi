## Description

Nine diamond diamonds pressed into a riveted block: to store them tightly or to build with some shine.

## How to get it

Nine diamonds on the crafting table's grid, or by itself in a storage with a compression upgrade. It comes apart into nine diamonds.

## Uses

Placed like a building block. Broken with a pickaxe.

## Behaviour

It does not burn and does not fall.

## Tips

With compression, a chest keeps nine times more diamond in the same room.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book shows how to make and unmake it.
