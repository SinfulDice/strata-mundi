## Description

Fine-grained creamy white terracotta, baked by centuries of sunshine.

## Obtaining

In creative: Nature tab of the inventory, or `/give white_terracotta`. In the world, it forms layers in the slopes and underground of the badlands.

## Usage

Warm-coloured walls, friezes, patterns: with its coloured cousins, it makes a real builder's palette.

## Behaviour

Full, opaque block, a bit harder than dirt.

## Tips

Each colour stays at the same height along a slope: follow the band you want.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a badlands layer to read its name at the top of the screen.
