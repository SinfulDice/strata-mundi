## Description

Water that got a little too chilly. Ice is translucent and bluish, hinting at what lies beneath, like a display case laid over the lake.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give ice`. In the world, it covers the surface of the snowy tundra's waters: a single layer, at sea level, with liquid water right underneath.

## Usage

Skating rinks (no sliding yet), frosted windows, snow palaces. It also forms a natural bridge to walk across frozen lakes.

## Behaviour

A solid, **translucent** block: you can see through it, slightly tinted. It's safe to walk on. For now it neither melts nor makes you slip: very well-behaved ice.

## Tips

Break an ice block to open a hole in the frozen lake; the water underneath won't budge, since water doesn't flow yet.

## Discovery in game

Walk onto a tundra lake and look down at your feet: the block's name appears at the top of the screen.
