## Description

A diamond-bound barrel: 108 slots (12 rows of nine) and 4 upgrade slots. Its diamond border tells its tier at a glance.

## How to get it

In creative: Storage tab of the inventory. In survival: eight diamonds and glass around a gold-bound barrel, or **better**: a diamond storage kit used (right click) on a gold-bound barrel already placed, without emptying it.

## Uses

Right click opens it; the screen scrolls its rows (buttons under the grid, mouse wheel). Its upgrades (sorting, filter, compression, destruction, magnet) go in the panel on the right. Like every barrel, it never joins its neighbours.

## Behaviour

Its contents and upgrades are saved with the world; broken, it drops everything, upgrades included.

## Tips

A magnet and a filter make a barrel that gathers by itself what lies around the mine.

## Discovery in game

The recipe book shows the kit and the barrel; an upgrade's tooltip says what it does.
