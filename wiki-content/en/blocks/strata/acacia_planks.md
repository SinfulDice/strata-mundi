## Description

Neatly lined-up acacia planks of bright orange wood.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or `/give acacia_planks`. They are not found in the world: this is a building block.

## Usage

Floors, walls, decks and timber frames; their colour sets them apart from oak and fir planks.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block.

## Tips

A floor alternating two woods instantly makes a room look tidy.

## Discovery in game

Search for "planks" in the creative inventory.
