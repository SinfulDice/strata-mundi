## Description

Glowing orange molten rock sleeping at the bottom of the world. It makes for splendid underground lakes… best admired from a distance.

## Obtaining

Lava **has no item**: you won't find it in the creative inventory, nor with `/give`. In the world, it fills caves, caverns and ravines at the very bottom, near the bedrock, in lakes with a flat surface.

## Usage

Scenery of the depths and a landmark: when you see it, the bedrock is not far below.

## Behaviour

Lava is **static**: it neither flows nor spreads. It isn't solid, so you pass through it like air; it doesn't burn yet. It can't be targeted or broken; however, you can **place a block in its place**.

## Tips

To cross a lava lake, place blocks on its surface and walk on them.

## Discovery in game

Since it can't be targeted, its name never appears at the top of the screen: you recognise it on sight when you go all the way down into the caves.
