## Description

A wooden stick crowned with a small flame. The torch is every explorer's companion: wherever it burns, night and caves step back.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or `/give torch`. It isn't found in nature.

## Usage

Lighting a cave, a mine tunnel, a house or a path. Leaving a trail of them behind you helps you find the way out.

## Behaviour

Gives off light of **level 14** (out of 15), which drops by one level per block travelled and goes around obstacles. It passes through air, water and glass, but not rock. It isn't solid and stands **on a solid block**: remove that block and the torch drops.

## Tips

One torch every ten to twelve blocks keeps a tunnel well lit. Placed at the edge of a block, its light spills easily into the next column.

## Discovery in game

It's waiting for you in the Building tab. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
