## Description

Deep green fir needles, packed into dense sprigs. They smell of winter and give the mountains their greeting-card silhouette.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give fir_leaves`. In the world, they form the **cone-shaped** crown of firs, on mountain slopes and in the snowy tundra.

## Usage

Hand-made festive trees, evergreen hedges, chalet roofs, artificial alpine forests.

## Behaviour

A solid but **see-through** block: you can see through the gaps between the sprigs, neighbouring needles included. Like oak leaves, they don't fall when you cut the trunk, and you can walk on them.

## Tips

For a realistic fir, stack ever-narrower layers of needles around a fir trunk, and finish with a single-block tip.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a fir's crown to read its name at the top of the screen. In the creative inventory, searching "needles" or "fir" finds it.
