## Description

Gold in deep stone: yellow nuggets that stand out all the more against the dark background. A little treasure of the depths.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give deep_gold_ore`. In the world, it replaces gold ore wherever a vein runs through deep stone. It's the form in which you'll meet gold most often.

## Usage

A decorative block for now. It **can't be harvested yet**: in creative, breaking it makes it vanish.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the deep stone family, and harder than ordinary gold ore (which will matter in survival).

## Tips

The contrast between the gold and the dark rock makes it easy to spot: bring a torch… well, keep your eyes peeled, light hasn't arrived yet.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. F3 tells you how deep you found it.
