## Description

A wooden chest with an iron latch, big enough for twenty-seven stacks of items. The must-have piece of furniture of any self-respecting home.

## How to get it

In creative: Storage tab of the inventory, or `/give chest`. Crafting arrives later.

## Uses

Right click opens it; while sneaking, right click puts a block against it. **Two chests side by side join** into a double chest of fifty-four slots. The inventory's "Stash" button drops into it at once what it already holds.

## Behaviour

Its contents are saved with the world. Broken, it drops everything inside. In a row of three, two chests join and the third stays single.

## Tips

One chest per family of items (stones, wood, ores) and the "Stash" button sorts for you in one click.

## Discovery in game

Open the Storage tab of the creative inventory, or type "chest" in the search.
