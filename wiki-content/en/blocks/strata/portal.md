## Description

A purple veil that swirls gently and glows in the dark. This **test portal** is purely decorative: it leads nowhere (for now).

## How to get it

In the creative inventory, Building tab.

## Behaviour

Gives light of **level 11**. You walk through it and see through it. Its texture is **animated**; the "Animated textures" graphics setting freezes it.

## Tips

Several portals side by side make a single sheet: the shared faces are not drawn.

## Discovering it in game

Place one in a dark cave and watch the purple glow on the stone.
