## Description

Stone is the backbone of the world: grey, sturdy, dependable. Dig through a few blocks of dirt, sand or snow and there it is, going on for a long, long way down.

## Obtaining

In creative, it waits for you in the Natural tab of the inventory, or through `/give stone`. In the world, it forms the core of every biome, under the surface layer. On the highest mountains it even pokes out into the open air, before snow takes over. Further down, it gives way to deep stone.

## Usage

The all-purpose building block: walls, foundations, towers that tickle the clouds. It also hosts the ore veins.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block. It doesn't fall, doesn't melt, doesn't move: serenity in block form. Caves wind through it in long twisting tunnels.

## Tips

A cave tunnel opening onto a cliff face is the best way to spot ores without digging up everything.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it: its name appears at the top of the screen. With F3, the "Targeted block" line also gives its identifier. A middle click puts it straight into your hotbar.
