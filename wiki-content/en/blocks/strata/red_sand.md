## Description

Orange-red sand, warmed by the sun of the badlands.

## Obtaining

In creative: Nature tab of the inventory, or `/give red_sand`. In the world, it covers the flat tops of the badlands.

## Usage

Colourful deserts, paths, Mars-inspired gardens.

## Behaviour

Full, opaque block. Like regular sand, it doesn't fall yet.

## Tips

A few blocks under the red sand, the terracotta strata of the badlands begin.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
