## Description

The pale, fibrous stem of a giant mushroom, soft like damp cardboard (but nicer).

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give mushroom_stem`. In the world, it holds up the cap of giant red and brown mushrooms.

## Usage

Pale columns, pillars for mushroom houses, unusual fences.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, soft block: it breaks quickly.

## Tips

Add a red or brown cap on top and you have your own giant mushroom.

## Discovery in game

Aim at the stem of a giant mushroom to read its name at the top of the screen.
