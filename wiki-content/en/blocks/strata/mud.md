## Description

Dark, sticky mud that makes lovely squelching noises when you walk in it (well, once sounds arrive).

## Obtaining

In creative: Nature tab of the inventory, or `/give mud`. In the world, it lines the floor and low banks of swamps.

## Usage

Swamp floors, wet gardens, stilt-house scenery.

## Behaviour

Full, opaque, soft block.

## Tips

Where the mud starts, the shallow water starts: handy for following a swamp's edge.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
