## Description

Sky-blue crystals set into deep stone, shining like stars in an ink-black sky. This is the form in which diamond truly shows itself in the world.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give deep_diamond_ore`. In the world, it lies at the very bottom, just above bedrock, in very small, very rare veins. With the base pack, the whole diamond zone is in deep stone: so this is the variant you'll find while exploring.

## Usage

A decorative block for now, and the most prestigious of all. It **can't be harvested yet**: in creative, breaking it makes it vanish.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the deep stone family, and harder than ordinary diamond ore (which will matter in survival).

## Tips

Caves that reach down to bedrock are your best friends: follow their lower walls, watching for the faintest blue glint.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen; F3 reminds you that you're very close to the bottom of the world.
