## Description

A birch trunk with white bark streaked with black, with pale, almost creamy rings at each end.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give birch_log`. In the world, it forms the trunks of birch trees in **forests**, among the oaks (and soon in birch forests): a tree tall and slender, with a narrow round crown.

## Usage

Beams, pillars, timber frames and cabins, with a colour of its own to vary your builds.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque block. Like every log, it is **oriented**: placed on the top or bottom of a block, it stands upright; placed against the side of a block, it lies down along the axis of the clicked face.

## Tips

Mix it with other woods to draw patterns on your walls.

## Discovery in game

Aim at a trunk to read its name at the top of the screen; a middle click gives you a copy.
