## Description

Oak planks in a warm golden brown, nicely planed, with even grain. The building wood par excellence: you already want to lay a rug on them.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or `/give oak_planks`. They don't appear in nature. Until crafting arrives, the creative inventory is their only source.

## Usage

Floors, walls, roofs, imaginary furniture, cosy houses. Paired with oak logs, they give an instant cottage look.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block. It neither burns nor rots for now: your wooden houses are safe from everything except your own renovations.

## Tips

Alternate oak and fir planks for patterned floors: the light-dark contrast instantly looks more polished.

## Discovery in game

Open the Building tab of the creative inventory, or type "oak" in the search to see log, planks and leaves side by side.
