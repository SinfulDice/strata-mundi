## Description

Stone studded with sparkling sky-blue crystals: diamond, the rarest and most coveted of ores. Finding it is a bit like finding a four-leaf clover underground.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give diamond_ore`. In the world, diamond is only found at the very bottom, just above bedrock, in very small veins. With the base pack, that whole zone lies in deep stone: so you'll almost always meet **deep diamond ore** there, and rarely if ever this stone version. It remains available for building and for packs that change the world's layers.

## Usage

A decorative block for now, perfect for a throne or a treasure room. It **can't be harvested yet**: breaking it in creative makes it vanish.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as stone.

## Tips

To see a real diamond vein, head down to the very bottom of the world and look for its deep variant.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or search "diamond" in the creative inventory to see both variants side by side.
