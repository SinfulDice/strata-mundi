## Description

Iron from the depths: rusty flecks on a deep stone background. Harder to spot than its stone cousin, but just as promising.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give deep_iron_ore`. In the world, it replaces iron ore wherever a vein runs through deep stone, in the depths of every biome.

## Usage

A decorative block for now. It **can't be harvested yet**: in creative, breaking it makes it vanish.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the deep stone family, and harder than ordinary iron ore (which will matter in survival).

## Tips

In deep caves, iron is one of the most frequent ores: if you're looking for diamond, you'll meet plenty of iron along the way.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen; the word "Deep" confirms you're in deep stone.
