## Description

Stone sprinkled with small rust-coloured and beige flecks: iron. Low-key but widespread, it's the serious member of the gang, the one that will go far.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or `/give iron_ore`. In the world, it forms small veins in stone, over a wide range of heights, from the depths to well up under the hills. A little less common than coal. In deep stone, it becomes deep iron ore.

## Usage

A decorative block for now: ores **can't be harvested yet**, and breaking one in creative simply makes it vanish. It will come into its own with survival.

## Behaviour

A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as stone.

## Tips

Long cave walls are the ideal place to spot several veins at a glance.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. F3 shows the altitude where you found it, useful for comparing with other ores.
