## Description

A green vine hanging under the foliage, like a garland the forest forgot.

## Obtaining

In creative: the Plants tab of the inventory, or `/give vine`. In the world, it hangs one to four blocks long under the edge of the crowns of great **jungle** trees and **swamp** oaks.

## Usage

Decorating walls, bridges and arches, giving a garden a wild look.

## Behaviour

A **crossed** plant: two crossing planes visible from every side, with **no collision** (you walk through it). It cannot be climbed yet.

## Tips

A few vines under a balcony are enough to make it look like a forgotten ruin.

## Discovery in game

Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or open the Plants tab of the creative inventory.
