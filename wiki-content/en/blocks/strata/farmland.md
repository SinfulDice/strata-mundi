## Description

Earth turned into dark, loose furrows, ready for crops.

## How to get it

Right click with a hoe on dirt or a grass block whose top is open to the air. Broken, it gives dirt back.

## Uses

Crops will grow on it once farming arrives; for now it decorates vegetable patches and gardens.

## Behaviour

It does not burn or fall. A hoe does nothing to dirt covered by a block.

## Tips

Lay strips of farmland between hedges to prepare your future fields.

## Discovery in game

The hoe's tooltip says that a right click works the aimed block.
