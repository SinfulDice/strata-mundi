## Description

A gold miner's hammer: it digs what a pickaxe digs, slower, but its head can be widened at the anvil to break a whole area at once.

## Obtaining

Five materials around a stick, a second stick below, at the crafting table.

## Usage

At the anvil, the **wide head** unlocks 3×1, 5×1, 3×3, 3×5 then 5×5, dearer each time. The "Hammer area" key picks a smaller area already unlocked. See the "Upgrades and anvil" page.

## Behaviour

Only the blocks the hammer can mine go with the block aimed at; the break lasts as long as the hardest; each block costs one use; the loot falls in one heap.
