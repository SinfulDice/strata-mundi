## TEH IDEA

IN SURVIVL, EVRY THING HAZ ITS SPOT: **27 BAG SPOTZ**, TEH **QUICK BAR** OV 9 (AT TEH BOTTOM), **4 ARMUR SPOTZ** AN TEH **OTHR PAW**. STUFFZ PILE UP (64 4 MOST BLOKZ, 1 4 TOOLZ AN BOATZ); A TOOL DAT WEARZ SHOWZ A LIL BAR. TEH SERVR DECIDEZ EVRYTHIN.

## OPEN AN PLAI

TEH INVENTORY KEY OPENZ IT (IN CRAYTIV, TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX OPENZ). IN TEH SCREEN:
- **LEFT CLIK**: GRAB, PUT, OR SWAP; U CAN ALSO **DRAG** FROM WUN SPOT 2 ANOTHR;
- **RITE CLIK**: GRAB HALF, OR PUT WUN;
- **SHIFT + CLIK**: SEND 2 TEH OTHR SIDE;
- **DUBL CLIK**: GATHR ALL TEH SAEM STUFFZ;
- **1 2 9** OVR A SPOT: SWAP WIF DAT BAR SPOT;
- **CLIK OUTSIDE** OR TEH YEET KEY: YEET IT ON TEH FLOOR.

WIF A CONTROLLR: A GRABZ OR PUTZ, X HALF, Y SENDZ, R3 GATHRZ, L3 YEETZ, L1 LOCKZ, B CLOSEZ.

## COMFY

- **SORT** BY KIND OR NAEM.
- **LOCK** A SPOT (MIDDL CLIK OR L1): SORT AN STASH NO TUCH IT.
- **STASH IT** PUTZ BAG STUFFZ IN NEER BOXEZ DAT ALREDDY HAZ DEM.
- TEH **SEARCH** OV A BOX MAEKZ TEH WRONG STUFFZ DARK.

## STUFFZ ON TEH FLOOR

TEH YEET KEY YEETZ WUT U HOLD (WIF SPRINT: TEH WHOLE PILE). A BROKD BLOK IN SURVIVL DROPZ ITSELF. FLOOR STUFFZ SPIN, JOIN WIF SAEM STUFFZ, GET **PIKD UP AUTO** WEN U WALK ON DEM AN **GO POOF AFTR 5 MINZ**. DEY STAY WIF TEH WURLD.

## BOXEZ AN BARRULZ

RITE CLIK OPENZ. 2 BOXEZ SIDE BY SIDE = WUN BIG BOX; BARRULZ STAY ALONE. BREAK IT AN TEH STUFFZ FALL OUT.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH HALP AT TEH BOTTOM OV TEH INVENTORY SCREEN SHOWZ EVRY MOOV WIF UR KEYZ.
