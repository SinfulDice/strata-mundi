## HOW IT WERKZ

A **HOT BOX** COOKZ TEH STUFF ON TOP WIF BURNY STUFF BELOW, AN TEH RESULT SHOWZ ON TEH RITE. TEH FLAYM SHOWZ BURNY STUFF LEFT, TEH ARROW TEH COOKIN. IT KEEPZ WERKIN WIF SCREEN CLOSD, AN ITS FACE GLOWZ WHILE FIRE BURNZ. **SUPR HOT BOX** ONLY COOKZ ROCK STUFFZ AN **SMOKY BOX** ONLY NOMZ, 2 TIEMZ FASTR.

## BURNY STUFF

COAL AN WOOD COAL: 8 COOKZ IN ROCK HOT BOX; HOT JUICE BUKKIT: 100 (BUKKIT COMEZ BAK); WOOD, PLANKZ, CRAFTIN TABUL, TREZUR BOX: 1 AN A HALF; STIK: HALF. FIRE ONLY STARTZ IF THER IZ STUFF 2 COOK AN ROOM 4 RESULT.

## UPGRAYDABUL HOT BOXEZ

A **KIT** (RITE CLIK) ON A HOT BOX MAKEZ IT NEXT TIER **RITE THER**: ROCK → IRON → GOLD → SHINY. STUFF INSIDE STAYZ. EACH TIER COOKZ FASTR (10, 8, 5, 3 SEKONDZ), GETZ MOAR FROM COAL (8, 10, 12, 16) AN GETZ UPGRAYD SPOTZ (0 2 3) 4 **CHIPZ**: ZOOM, LES BURNY, DUBBL STUFF. 2 SAME CHIPZ NO ADD UP. SAME KIT WERKZ ON SMOKY BOX AN SUPR HOT BOX.

## ROCK CHOPPA

PUT A BLOK IN ITS SPOT, PIK A LOOK IN TEH LIST (ROCK BRIKZ, SMOOF ROCK, CHOPPD SANDZ ROCK…) AN TAEK IT. NO BURNY STUFF, NOTHIN LOST: 1 BLOK = 1 BLOK.
