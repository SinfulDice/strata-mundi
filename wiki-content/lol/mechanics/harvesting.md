## HOW LONG

IT DEPENDZ ON TEH BLOK **HARDNES** AN TEH **TOOL** U HOLD: EVRY BLOK HAZ A FAVRIT TOOL (DIGGY STIK, CHOPPY, SCOOPY STIK, SNIPPY). WIF PAWZ, A BLOK TAEKZ HARDNES × 1.5 SECZ (DIRTZ: 0.75 SECZ); TEH RITE TOOL IZ FASTR. EVRY BLOK PAEG SAYZ ITS HARDNES, ITS TOOL AN ITS TIEM WIF PAWZ.

## U NEEDZ A TOOL

SUM BLOKZ (ROKZ, SHINY ROKZ, SHINY DARK ROCK…) **NEEDZ** A TOOL: WIFOUT, OR WIF A TOOL OV TOO LOW **LEVUL**, IT IZ SUPR SLOW (HARDNES × 5 SECZ) AN U GETZ **NUTHIN**. TEH LIL BOX ON TEH STUFF SAYZ IT. LEVULZ: 0 WOOD, 1 ROK, 2 IRON, 3 SHINY.

## LOOTZ

A BLOK BROKD IN SURVIVL DROPZ ITS LOOTZ. MOSTLY IT IZ TEH BLOK ITSELF; SUM GIV OTHR STUFFZ, FROM A **LOOTZ TABUL** IN TEH PAK: GRAS GIVZ DIRTZ, LEEFS AN TALL GRAS NEEDZ SNIPPY, MUSHRUM BLOKZ SUMTIEMZ GIV MUSHRUMZ, SEE-THRU BLOK AN ICE GO SMASH. TEH DICE FOLLOW TEH WURLD SEED.
