## HOW IT WERKZ

**HOT FIRE** IZ A BLOK OV FLAEMZ DAT GIVEZ LITE (LEVUL 15), GETZ OLD AN JUMPZ AROUND. IT SITZ ON A SOLID BLOK OR NEXT 2 A BURNY 1. EVRY STEP (A BIT MOAR DAN 1 AN A HALF SECONDZ), IT GETZ OLDR, MAI **NOM** A BURNY NAYBUR (SUMTIEMZ LEEVIN A FLAEM THAR) AN **LITE TEH AIR** AROUND, UP 2 4 BLOKZ HIGH, WEN DAT AIR TOUCHEZ SUMTHIN BURNY. OLDR FIRE = LESS JUMPIN; FIRE WIF NUTHIN 2 NOM GOEZ OUT SOON, AN EVRY BIG FIRE STOPZ BY ITSELF.

## SPARKY ROKZ (CRAYTIV)

IN TEH **TOOLZ** TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, **SPARKY ROKZ** LITEZ A FIRE WIF A **RITE CLICK** ON TEH SIDE U LOOK AT, IF FIRE CAN SIT THAR (NOT NEXT 2 WATUR). TEH SERVR CHEKZ EVRY USE (HOW FAR, FREE SPOT), LIEK 4 PUTTIN A BLOK.

## WUT BURNZ

- **LOGZ**: HARD 2 LITE, BURN SLOW.
- **PLANKZ**: A BIT FASTR.
- **LEEFZ**: CATCH EZ.
- **GRAS, FERNZ, BUSHEZ, FLOWRZ**: GO WHOOSH SUPR FAST.
- **VINEZ**: BURN FAST BUT LITE LIL AIR AROUND.

ROCK, DIRTZ, SAND AN SHROOMZ NO BURN. EVRY BLOK PAGE SEZ IF IT IZ **BURNY**.

## STOPPIN IT

- **WATUR** STOPZ A FIRE DAT TOUCHEZ IT RITE AWAY, AN NO FIRE LITEZ NEXT 2 IT.
- BREAK TEH FIRE OR PUT A BLOK THAR = NO MOAR FIRE.
- WIF NUTHIN 2 NOM, IT GOEZ OUT BY ITSELF IN SUM SECONDZ.

## SETTINZ

WURLD SETTINZ, PICKD WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD OR WIF `/fire`: `spread` (FIRE MAEKZ NEW FIREZ) AN `destroy` (BURNY STUFF IZ NOMMED). FIREZ SHARE TEH UPDAETZ PER TICK OV WATR AN LAVA (`/fluids <budget>`).
