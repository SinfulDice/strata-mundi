## SHINY STUFFZ

NEXT 2 ARMR, POCKETZ GOT 5 SPOTZ: **2 LOOPZ**, **1 NECK SHINY**, **1 BELTZ** AN **1 CAPE**. SPESHUL FOOT BOXEZ GO IN FOOT SPOT. MAEK LOOPZ, NECK SHINY AN BELTZ AT TEH **SHINY MAEKR**, CAPE AT TEH **STRING WEEVR**:

- **LOOP OF MOAR LIEF**: 4 MOAR LIEF.
- **NECK SHINY OF HOLD BREFF**: 5 MOAR SECONDZ OV BREFF IN WATR.
- **BOTTUL BELTZ**: SPOTZ 4 BOTTULZ, 4 LATR.
- **FLOOF CAPE**: NO COLD (LATR).

## NO STACKIN

**SAEM FING NO STACK**: 2 SAEM LOOPZ COUNT ONCE. ONLY TEH STRONGEST COUNTZ.

## MATCHYZ

WEAR MATCHY BITZ OV SAEM STUFF = **MATCHY BONUS**, BIGGR WIF 2, 3 OR 4 BITZ. HOVR BOX SAYZ HOW MANY U WEAR (2/4…) AN WICH BONUS IZ ON. BROKED BIT NO COUNT.

## ZOOMIES FROM STUFFZ

- **BOING FOOT BOXEZ**: JUMP AGAIN IN TEH SKY.
- **ZOOMIES BELTZ**: SMOL ZOOM WIF "ZOOMIES" BUTTON (F, CROSS DOWN), DEN NAP.
- **POKEY FOOT BOXEZ**: WALK IN WALL = CLIMB IT; CROUCH 2 HOLD ON.

FOOT BOXEZ AT TEH **HOT BANGR**, BELTZ AT SHINY MAEKR. WURLD OPSHUN "JUMPY BOOTZ" TURNZ DEM ALL OFF.
