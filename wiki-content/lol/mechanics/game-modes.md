## HOW IT WORKZ

TEH GAEM MOAD PIKZ TEH RULEZ OV A WURLD. U PIK IT WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD, IN TEH "NU WURLD" SCREEN, AN IT SHOWZ IN TEH WURLDZ LIST. 2 MOADZ: **CRAYTIV** AN **SURVIVL**. 4 NAO, `/gamemode survival` PUTZ A WURLD IN SURVIVL.

## CRAYTIV MOAD

TEH SANDBOX WIF NO RULEZ, 2 BILD AN EXPLOR AS U WANT:
- **ALL TEH BLOKZ** R IN TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, WIF TABZ AN SEARCH;
- U **BREAK STUFFZ IN ONE CLIK**, EVEN BOTTUM ROCK;
- U **FLY** WIF 2 QUIK JUMPZ;
- ALL TEH CONSOLE **COMMANDZ** R OK;
- U **KNOW EVRYTHIN** ALREDDY: NOTHIN 2 FIND OUT BEFOR U BILD.

## SURVIVL

NO MOAR FLYIN, BLOKZ U PUT COM FROM UR STUFFZ, AN A BROKD BLOK DROPZ ITSELF ON TEH FLOOR. WALK ON IT 2 PIK IT UP. BOTTUM ROCK STAYZ UNBREAKBUL. MOAR RULEZ COM DURIN V0.3.

## CHANGIN MOAD

`/gamemode creative` AN `/gamemode survival` CHANGE TEH MOAD; IT IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH "NU WURLD" SCREEN SHOWZ TEH MOAD, TEH WURLDZ LIST SHOWZ TEH MOAD OV EACH WURLD, AN `/help gamemode` XPLAINZ TEH COMMAND.
