## HOW IT WERKZ

IN STAYIN ALIVE, U GET **HOT OR COLD**. TEH FEELY HOTNESS SHOWZ OVR TEH HOTBAR ON TEH LEFT: A **SCALE** FROM BLU (SO FROZE) TO RED (SO SPICY), COZY IN TEH MIDDL, WIF A WHITE MARK WER U IZ. WEN NOT COZY, A LINE SEZ TEH ZONE AN DEGREEZ ("COLDZ 3 °C"); WEN IT GETZ WORSE, A TIP SHOWZ 4 A BIT.

TEH FEELY HOTNESS FOLLOWZ WAT IZ AROUND U, SLOWLY. IT ADDZ:

- TEH **PLACE** (DESRT IZ SPICY BY DAY, SNOWY PLACE IZ FROZE);
- TEH **NITE**, COLDER EVRYWER, AN SUPR COLD IN DESRT;
- **HIGHNESS**: MOAR UP, MOAR COLD;
- **WATR**: SWIMMIN COOLZ U, AN U STAY **WETZ** 4 A WHILE (U DRY 3 TIEMS FASTR NEAR FIRE);
- TEH **WARMZ** OF BLOKZ NEAR: FIRE, LAVA, FIRE STIKZ AN GLOWY BOXEZ (RLY CLOSE), HOT OVENZ, TEH **CAMP FIREZ**; ONLY TEH STRONGEST COUNTZ;
- A **ROOF**: KEEPZ HALF TEH WARMZ AT NITE AN GIVZ SHADE BY DAY;
- **CLOTHEZ**: TEH WOOL CAPE AN TEH CLOTH AN LEATHR SETZ KEEP COLD OUT (EACH POINT = 3 DEGREEZ CLOSER 2 COZY, NEVR PAST). WOOLZ IZ ALSO WARM IN DESRT, LOL!

## WAT IT DOEZ

- **COLDZ**: U GO SLOW AN GET HUNGRIER.
- **HOTZ**: U GET THIRSTY AN TIRED FASTR.
- **SO FROZE** AN **SO SPICY**: U ALSO LOSE HP SLOWLY. NO FROZE AN NO BURN STATZ MAKE IT LESS OUCH.

TEH SCREEN EDGEZ GO FROSTY OR GLOWY: TURN IT OFF IN OPSHUNZ (INTERFACE). U CAN TURN HOT AN COLD OFF WEN MAKIN TEH WORLD; IN CREATIV, U FEEL NOTHIN.

## TIPZ

A CAMP FIREZ AN A WOOL CAPE IZ ENUF 4 A NITE IN SNOW. IN DESRT, WALK AT DAWN, IN SHADE OR IN WATR, AN KEEP A WATR BAG.
