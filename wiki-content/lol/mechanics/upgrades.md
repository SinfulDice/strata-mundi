## TEH BONK STIK

DIGZ LIEK DIGGY STIK, SLOWR, BUT ITS HED GETZ BIGGR AT TEH BONK BLOK: 3×1, 5×1, 3×3, 3×5 DEN 5×5. ONLY BLOKZ IT CAN DIG GO. IT TAEKZ AZ LONG AZ TEH HARDEST BLOK. EACH BLOK USEZ TEH STIK. LOOT FALLZ IN 1 PILE. "BONK SIZE" KEY (R, RITE STIK CLIK) PIKZ A SMALLR 1. U SEE TEH BOX ROUND TEH BLOK.

## FIXIN
