## WUT IZ DIS

A **FLOATEE** FLOATZ ON TOP OV WATUR AN HOT JUICE, AN NEVR SINKZ. IT COMEZ IN **OAK**, **POINTY TREE**, **WHITE TREE**, **JUNGUL** AN **FLAT TREE**: TEH BOAT IZ MADE OV TEH PLANKZ OV ITZ TREE. IN MAKIN MODE, DEY R IN TEH **GO PLACEZ** TAB OV TEH STUFF BOX.

## PUT, GIT IN, GIT OFF

- **RITE CLIK** WIF A FLOATEE ON WATUR (OR ON TEH GROUND): IT GOEZ DOWN, LOOKIN WER U LOOK.
- **RITE CLIK** ON A FREE FLOATEE: U SIT IN IT. TEH CAMRA FOLLOWZ TEH FLOATEE, U CAN STILL LOOK AROUND.
- **SNEAK** (KEY U CAN CHANGE, GAEMPAD 2): U GIT OFF, ON TEH SHORE NEXT 2 IT IF CLOSE, OR ELSE ON TOP OV TEH FLOATEE. TEH GAEM TELLZ U AT TEH BOTTOM OV TEH SCREEN WHILE U SIT.
- IN MAKIN MODE, **LEFT CLIK** ON A FLOATEE TAEKZ IT AWAY.

## DRIVIN

- **GO** AN **BAK** PADDUL; GOIN BAK IZ WAI SLOWR.
- **LEFT** AN **RITE** TURN TEH FLOATEE.
- ON WATUR, TEH FLOATEE GOEZ FAST AN SLIDEZ A BIT BEFOAR STOPPIN.
- ON DRY GROUND, IT GOEZ **SLOW** AN STOPZ RITE AWAY.
- ON **ICE**, IT SLIDEZ ALMOST 4EVR AN GOEZ **SUPR FAST**.
- IT STOPZ AGAINST BLOKZ, AN A SHORE HIGHR DAN TEH WATUR HOLDZ IT BAK.

## SAVIN

FLOATEEZ R SAVED WIF TEH WORLD, AN U R BAK IN UR SEAT IF U LEFT WHILE SITTIN IN 1.
