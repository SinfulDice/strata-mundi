## HOW IT WERKZ

TOOLZ BREAK BLOKZ FASTR AN LET U KEEP TEH ONEZ DAT NEEDZ DEM. EVRY TOOL HAZ A **TIER**: **WOOD, ROCK, IRON, GOLD, SHINY** (MOAR LATR). TEH TIER SEZ TEH **DIGGIN LEVUL** (0 WOOD, 1 ROCK, 2 IRON AN GOLD, 3 SHINY), TEH **ZOOM**, TEH **USEZ**, TEH **OUCHIEZ** AN TEH **BETTR SLOTZ**. SHINY NEEDZ IRON DIGGY STIK, SHINY DARK ROCK NEEDZ SHINY DIGGY STIK. GOLD IZ SUPR FAST BUT BREAKZ QUIK.

## TOOLZ

- **DIGGY STIK, CHOPPY, SCOOPY STIK**: ROCKZ, WOOD, DIRTZ.
- **DIRT SCRATCHR**: RITE CLIK ON DIRTZ WIF NOTHIN ON TOP = DIRT 4 FOODZ.
- **STABBY**: KEEPZ ITS OUCHIEZ 4 FITEZ LATR.
- **SNIPPY**: KEEPZ LEEFZ AN TALL GRAS.
- **GRAS CHOPPA**: CHOP 1 PLANT, ALL PLANTZ AROUND GO 2, 1 USE ONLY.
- **ROCK POKR**: RITE CLIK ON ROCK BRIKZ = FANCY, THEN SWIRLY, THEN PLAIN AGAIN.
- **ALL-IN-WAN STIK**: DIGGY + CHOPPY + SCOOPY, IRON LEVUL, BREAKZ SOONR.

WOOD AN ROCK TOOLZ U KNOW FROM START.

## CLANKY CLOTHEZ

4 SLOTZ: HED, CHEST, LEGZ, FEETZ. 3 **WEIGHTZ**:

- **FLOOFY** (LETHR, CLOFF): LIL SHIELDY, NO PROBLEM;
- **MEDIUM** (CLINKY, SKALEZ): MOAR SHIELDY, LATR COSTZ LIL ENERGEE;
- **CHONKY** (PLAETZ): MOST SHIELDY, BUT EVRY PIECE MAEKS U **WALK SLOWR** (2.5 %, 10 % 4 ALL).

IRON AN GOLD COME CLINKY AN PLAET, SHINY COMEZ SKALEZ AN PLAET. U SEE TEH TOTAL **SHIELDY** IN UR STUFFZ.

## USEZ

EVRY USE WEARZ TEH TOOL; TEH LIL BAR SHOWZ WUT IZ LEFT. WEN U MAEK A WURLD, "OLD STUFFZ" CHOOZEZ: IT **BREAKZ** AT ZERO, OR U **KEEP IT 4 FIXIN**, GRAY AN USELESS.

## TEMPORARY STUFFZ

TALL GRAS AN FERNZ CHOPPD WIFOUT SNIPPY SUMTIMEZ GIVE **GRAS STRINGZ**. 3 STRINGZ = **CLOFF**; 2 STRINGZ + 1 SQUISHY DIRTZ = **LETHR** 4 NAO, TIL TEH AMINALZ COME.
