## TEH BARZ

IN SURVIVL, U HAS **LIFEZ** (20 POINTZ, 2 PER HART; TEH LIFE RING GIVEZ MOAR), **HUFF PUFF**, **HUNGRY** AN **THIRSTY**. LIFEZ, HUFF PUFF AN MAGIC (LATR) R TOP LEFT; HUNGRY AN THIRSTY R OVR TEH STUFF ROW. UNDR WATR U SEE TEH **BREATH** BAR. U CAN MOOV ALL BARZ IN TEH HUD MAKER. NO SHINY CLOTHEZ BAR: LOOK IN TEH BAG.

- **HUNGRY**: RUNNIN, JUMPIN, SWIMMIN AN DIGGIN MAEK U TIRD; TIRD EATZ TEH SECRIT **FULLZ** FURST, DEN HUNGRY. WELL FED, LIFEZ COMEZ BAK BY ITSELF; STARVIN, LIFEZ GOEZ SLOW, BUT HUNGRY ALONE NEVR KILLZ.
- **THIRSTY**: IT GOEZ DOWN WIF TIEM AN WERK. AT ZERO, LIFEZ GOEZ SLOW.
- **HUFF PUFF**: RUNNIN, JUMPIN AN ZOOMIN USE IT; IT COMEZ BAK FAST. OUT OV PUFF, NO RUNNIN TIL IT COMEZ BAK A BIT. HEVY CLOTHEZ MAEK IT SMOLR. 2 HUNGRY = NO RUNNIN.
- **BREATH**: UNDR WATR IT GOEZ DOWN; AT ZERO, BLUB BLUB. TEH SHORT BREATH NEKLACE GIVEZ MOAR.

WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD, U CAN TURN OFF HUNGRY, THIRSTY AN HUFF PUFF, AN PIK HOW FAST (25 % TO 200 %). IN CREATIF, NOTHIN GOEZ DOWN AN NOTHIN HURTZ.

## NOMZ, SLURPZ, FIXIN

**HOLD TEH RITE BUTTON** (RITE TRIGGR ON TEH PAD) WIF TEH STUFF IN PAW. NO NOMZ WEN FULL. SOUP GIVEZ BAK TEH **BOWL**, BOTTL GIVEZ BAK TEH EMPTEE BOTTL.

- **WATR**: A **GLAS BOTTL** OR A **SKIN BAG** FILLZ WIF RITE CLIK ON WATR. BY TEH SEA TEH WATR IZ **SALTY**: SLURPIN IT MAEKZ U MOAR THIRSTY; BOIL IT IN TEH HOT BOX. CROUCHIN WIF EMPTEE PAW, U CAN SLURP RITE FROM TEH WATR. SKIN BAG HAZ 4 SLURPZ.
- **NOMZ**: BERRIZ (BUSHEZ CUT WIFOUT SIKL), APULZ (OAK LEEFZ CUT WIFOUT SNIPPERZ), HOT APULZ AN CRISPY SHROOMZ (SMOKR OR HOT BOX), SHROOM SOUP. BRED COMEZ WIF FARMIN, MEET WIF AMINALZ.
- **FIXIN**: **OUCHIE WRAP** AN **BOO-BOO BOX** STOP TEH LEAKIN AN FIX U A FEW SECONDZ. SAME MAGIC NEVR STAX: ONLY TEH STRONGR FIXIN COUNTZ.
- A **BAKPAK WIF TEH FEEDIN UPGRAYD** FEEDZ U BY ITSELF, ONLY WEN NOTHIN IZ WASTD.

## OWIES

FALLIN (MOAR DAN 3 BLOKZ), BLUB BLUB, HOT GOO, HOTZ (AN STILL ON FIRE TIL U JUMP IN WATR), HED IN A BLOK, HUNGRY, THIRSTY, LEAKIN. SHINY CLOTHEZ HELP FOR BONKZ AN FALLZ, NOT HUNGRY OR HOT GOO. AFTR A OWIE, U R SAFE A TINY TIEM. TEH SCREEN GOEZ RED AN WOBBLEZ: BOTH IN TEH OPSHUNZ.

AT ZERO LIFEZ, FOR NAO, U **FAINT** AN WAKE UP AT TEH START WIF ALL UR STUFF. REEL DED COMEZ LATR.

## FIND OUT IN TEH GAEM

TEH TOOLTIPZ OV NOMZ, SLURPZ AN FIXIN SAY WUT DEY GIVE; EMPTEE BOTTLZ SAY WER 2 FILL.
