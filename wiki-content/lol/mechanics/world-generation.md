## HOW IT WORKZ

TEH WURLD IZ NOT DRAWED BY PAW: IT IZ **CALCULATED** FROM A SEED, BIT BY BIT WHILE U WALK. SAEM SEED, SAEM WURLD, BLOK BY BLOK, IN ANY ORDR. IT IZ KINDA INFINIT: ONLY TEH WURLD EDGE U PIKD STOPZ IT.

## GROUND AN BIOMZ

4 INVIZIBUL MAPZ COVR TEH WURLD: **HOT OR COLD**, **WET OR DRY**, **HOW FAR FROM TEH SEA** AN **HOW WORN DOWN** (FROM OLD FLAT LAND 2 YUNG LAND). TEH LAST 2 GIVE TEH GROUND HEIGHT: DEEP BIG WATUR, SOFT HILLZ, POINTY BIG ROCKZ. TEH BIOM, PIKD FROM TEH WEATHR KIND, ONLY DECIDEZ WUT IZ ON TOP AN WUT GROWZ: GOIN FROM ONE 2 ANOTHR IZ SOFT, NO SCARY CLIFFZ. 6 BIOMZ 4 NAO.

## UNDR TEH GROUND

UNDR TEH BIOM DIRTZ COMEZ ROCK, DEEP ROCK, DEN BOTTUM ROCK. WATUR FILLZ TEH LOW PLAECEZ UP 2 WATUR LINE. WIGGLY **DARK HOLEZ** GO THRU TEH ROCK BUT NEVR OPEN UNDR TEH SEA, **IN TEH ROCK STUFFZ** MAEK LIL STRIPEZ DEPENDIN ON DEEPNESS, AN TREEZ AN POKEY PLANTZ GROW DEPENDIN ON TEH BIOM.

## WER U START

FLAT GRAS OR LOTZ OF TREEZ, ON DRY GROUND, WIF WATUR CLOSE.
