## WUT IZ DIS

WATUR AN HOT JUICE **FLOWZ**. EVRY WET STUFF HAZ **SAUCEZ** (TEH WATUR OV SEAZ AN LAEKZ, TEH HOT JUICE LAEKZ WAI DOWN) AN **FLOWZ** DAT GET MOAR AN MOAR THIN: DOWN FURST, DEN 2 TEH SIDEZ, 2 TEH CLOSEST SLOEP. WATUR GOEZ UP 2 7 BLOKZ ON FLAT GROUND, HOT JUICE ONLY 3, AN WAI SLOWR. TAEK AWAY TEH SAUCE AN TEH FLOW GOEZ AWAY. WATUR + HOT JUICE = **ROCK** OR **SHINY DARK ROCK**.

## WUT U SEEZ

TEH TOP OV WET STUFF **LEANZ** WIF TEH FLOW: FULL NEAR TEH SAUCE, ALMOST FLAT AT TEH END. 2 BLOKZ OV TEH SAEM WET STUFF NEVR HAZ A WALL BETWEEN, AN WET STUFF WIF MOAR OV ITSELF ON TOP FILLZ ALL ITZ BLOK (WATURFALLZ). WATUR IZ SEE-THRU, HOT JUICE NOT; TEH TOP **WIGGLEZ**, AN TEH GRAFIX OPSHUN "WIGGLY WATUR" STOPZ DAT.

## SWIMMIN

- U **SINK SLOW** WEN U TOUCH NOTHIN.
- **HOLD JUMP**: U GO UP, TIL U FLOAT AT TEH TOP.
- U GO **HALF AS FAST** IN WATUR, EVEN SLOWR IN HOT JUICE.
- NEXT 2 A SHORE, A **LIL KICK** HELPZ U GET OUT.
- HEAD IN WET STUFF = ALL **FOG**, BLU (WATUR) OR ORANJ (HOT JUICE).
- FLYIN IN CREATIF, WET STUFF NO BOTHR U.

## TEH BUKKIT (CREATIF)

IN TEH **TOOLZ** TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX:
- TEH **BUKKIT OF WATUR** AN TEH **BUKKIT OF HOT JUICE** PUTZ A SAUCE WIF **RITE CLIK**, ON TEH BLOK U LOOK AT;
- TEH EMPTY **BUKKIT**, ON A SAUCE, **TAEKZ IT BAK**.

IN CREATIF, TEH BUKKIT STAYZ TEH SAEM. TEH SERVR CHEKZ EVRY USE (HOW FAR, WICH BLOK), SAEM AS PUTTIN A BLOK.
