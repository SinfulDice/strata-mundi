## WUT IZ DIS

ON TERRA, A DAI IZ **20 MINUTZ**: ABOUT 10 MINUTZ OV SUNNY AN 10 OV NITE, WIF A SHORT PINKY TIEM WEN SUN COMEZ UP AN GOEZ DOWN. TEH **SUN** COMEZ UP ON TEH EAST SIDE, CROSSEZ TEH SKY LEANIN A LIL 2 TEH SOUTH, DEN TEH **MUN** TAEKZ OVR. HOW LONG TEH SUNNY BIT IZ AN HOW DARK NITE IZ COMEZ FROM TEH PAK OV TEH PLACE, SO A PAK CAN CHANGE THEM.

## WUT U SEEZ

- **TEH SKY** GOEZ FROM SUNNY BLU 2 ORANJ AN DEN 2 DARK BLU NITE. TEH FOG AN TEH FAR EDGE FOLLOW.
- **TEH SUN AN MUN** R BIG CRISPY PIXELZ. TEH MUN HAZ **8 SHAEPZ**, 1 PER DAI, FROM ROUND MUN 2 NO MUN AN BAK.
- **SHINY DOTZ** TWINKLE AT NITE AN GO AWAY NEAR TEH FAR EDGE AN IN TEH MORNIN.
- **FLUFFEHS** FLOAT BY SLOW, ALWAYS TEH SAEM WAI, WAI UP HIGH: ABOVE TEH HILLZ, BELOW TEH BIG ROCKZ. THEIR COLOR FOLLOWZ TEH TIEM (WHITE IN TEH DAI, PINKISH WEN SUN GOEZ DOWN, DARK GREY AT NITE). THEY ONLY MOOV WHILE TIEM PASSEZ: WEN GAEM IZ PAWSD, THEY STOP.
- **DAYLITE** GOEZ DOWN AT NITE BUT NEVR 2 ZERO: U CAN STILL SEE A LIL BIT. LITEZ U PUT (FIRE STIKZ, GLOWY BOXEZ, GLOWY ROCK) MAEK TEH DIFFRENCE.

## SETTIN TEH TIEM

- `/time` REEDZ OR SETZ TEH WURLD TIEM; `/daycycle` STOPZ OR STARTZ IT AGAIN. U TYPE BOTH IN CRAYTIV.
- WEN U MAEK A NU WURLD, TEH **DAI AN NITE** BUTTN PICKZ TIEM DAT PASSEZ OR TIEM DAT IZ STUK. IT IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD.
- TEH TIEM IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD. IT NEVR GOEZ BAK: TEH MUN SHAEPZ KEEP COUNTIN TEH DAIZ.
- TIEM STOPZ WEN TEH GAEM IZ PAWSD.

## SETTINGZ

2 GRAFIX OPSHUNZ R ABOUT TEH SKY (PIXELZ TAB):
- **SHINY DOTZ**: ON OR OFF. ALMOST FREE. OFF IN TEH "POTATO" PRESET.
- **FLUFFEHS**: **NO FLUFFEHS**, **FLAT FLUFFEHS** (1 FLAT LAYR, SUPR CHEEP) OR **FAT FLUFFEHS** (WIF VOLUME, GRAFIX CARD WURK BIT MOAR). "POTATO" TURNZ THEM OFF, "SMOL" AN "DS" KEEP THEM FLAT, TEH OTHRS USE FAT ONEZ.

CHANGEZ WORK RITE AWAY. TEH PAK OV TEH PLACE ALSO SETZ HOW LONG TEH DAI IZ, HOW MANY FLUFFEHS AN HOW FAST THEY GO.

## PRO TIPZ

- NITE FELL? IN CRAYTIV, `/time set day` SAEVZ U WAITIN 4 MORNIN.
- 2 BILD WIF SUN STUK: `/time set noon` DEN `/daycycle off`.
- A ROUND MUN NITE IZ TEH BRITEST 2 LOOK AT; COUNT 8 DAIZ FROM 1 ROUND MUN 2 TEH NEXT.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

U FINDZ TEH SKY JUS BY PLAIIN: SUN, MUN AN FLUFFEHS R THERE FROM TEH FURST DAI. `/help time` AN `/help daycycle` EXPLAIN TEH COMMANDZ, AN TEH OPSHUNZ MENU HAZ A LIL LINE 4 EACH SETTIN. [LORE À ÉCRIRE]: WUT TEH TERRA FOLKZ SAI ABOUT TEH SUN AN TEH MUN.
