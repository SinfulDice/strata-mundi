## HOW IT WORKZ

EACH WURLD HAZ ITZ OWN FOLDR: ITZ PAPERZ (NAEM, SEED, MOAD, PLAI TIEM), UR KITTEH (WER U R, WER U LOOK, TEH BAR) AN A PIKCHUR 4 TEH WURLDZ LIST. TEH GROUND CAN B MADE AGAIN FROM TEH SEED, SO **ONLY CHANGED PLAECEZ** R SAEVD: A WURLD U WALKD A LOT BUT DIDNT TOUCH MUCH STAYZ TEENY.

## WEN TEH GAEM SAEVZ

- **ALL ALONE**, EVRY FEW MINZ (CHANGE IT IN OPSHUNZ → GAEM).
- WIF **SAEV AN BAI**, IN TEH PAWS MENU.
- WEN U CLOZE TEH GAEM WINDOW.
- ON TEH DS, WEN U **CLOZE TEH LID**: PAWS AN SAEV RITE AWAY. LIEK A KITTEH FALLIN ASLEEP MID-STEP.

## NO BROKD WURLDZ EVR

EACH FILE IZ WRITTEN NEXT 2 TEH OLD ONE, DEN SWAPPD IN ONE GO: IF TEH POWR GOEZ OUT, U HAZ TEH OLD OR TEH NU ONE, NEVR A MESS. A FILE DAT CANT B REED IZ PUT ASIDE, NOT ERASED.

## BAKUPZ

EVRY TIEM U OPEN A WURLD, A FULL COPY IZ MADE; TEH NEWEST ONEZ R KEPT (HOW MANY IZ IN OPSHUNZ → GAEM). DELEETIN A WURLD KEEPZ ITZ BAKUPZ.

## VERSHUNZ

A WURLD FROM A WRONG GAEM VERSHUN IZ GRAY IN TEH LIST, WIF TEH REESON.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH WURLDZ LIST SHOWZ PIKCHUR, DAET, MOAD AN PLAI TIEM; TEH DELEET QUESTSHUN SAYZ BAKUPZ STAY.
