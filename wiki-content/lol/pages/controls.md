## KEYBORD, MOUSE AN GAEM PAD

STRATA MUNDI WORKZ JUS AS GUD WIF **KEYBORD AN MOUSE** (NOM) AS WIF A **GAEM PAD**, AN U CAN SWITCH ANY TIEM. TEH HINTZ ON SCREEN FOLLO TEH LAST THING U TOUCHD: KEYBORD KEY OR GAEM PAD BUTTON.

## ANY KEYBORD

TEH GAEM REEDZ **WER** TEH KEYZ R, NOT TEH LETTR ON DEM: ON A FRENCH AZERTY KEYBORD, WALKIN IZ ON Z, Q, S, D. BUT TEH TEXTZ SHOW TEH LETTR REELY ON UR KEY.

## TEH GAEM PAD

BUTTONZ HAZ NEW 3DS NAEMZ (A ON TEH RITE, B AT TEH BOTTUM, X AT TEH TOP, Y ON TEH LEFT), SAEM AS TEH DS HANDHELD. LEFT STIK 2 WALK, RITE STIK 4 TEH CAMRA, WIF A DED ZONE U CAN CHANGE. ALL TEH MENUZ WORK WIF TEH GAEM PAD, WIF A KEYBORD ON TEH SCREEN 4 TYPIN; ONLY TEH COMMANDZ CONSOLE NEEDZ A REAL KEYBORD.

## CHANGIN KEYZ

EVRY ACSHUN CAN B CHANGED IN **OPSHUNZ → KEYZ**, KEYBORD AN GAEM PAD: PIK TEH ACSHUN, PRES OK, DEN PRES TEH NU KEY. A KEY ALREDDY USED SUMWER ELSE GETZ REMOOVD DERE, AN A BUTTON PUTZ ALL TEH KEYZ BAK LIEK BEFOR. SPEEDZ, UPSIDE-DOWN LOOKIN AN AUTO JUMP R IN **OPSHUNZ → MOOVZ**.
