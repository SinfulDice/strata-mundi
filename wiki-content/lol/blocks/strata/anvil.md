## DESCRIPSHUN

BIG IRON BLOK WIF HORN AN FOOT: HERE U MAEK FINGZ BETTR AN FIX DEM.

## HOW 2 GETZ

7 IRON BARZ AT TEH CRAFTIN TABUL (3, 1 IN MIDDL, 3). DIG IT WIF DIGGY STIK. IN CRAYTIV: WERKIN BLOKZ TAB.

## WUT IZ 4

RITE CLIK 2 OPEN. PUT FING LEFT, STUFF RITE.

- **BETTR**: PIK IN TEH LIST (LOOK AT IT 4 LVL, WUT IT DOEZ AN COSTZ), DEN TAEK IT.
- **FIX**: NO PIK ("FIX" BUTTON): ITS STUFF GIVZ BAK A QUARTR OV USEZ EACH; SAEM FING AGAIN ADDZ ITS USEZ AN 12 % MOAR. BROKED FINGZ KEPT GETZ FIXD 2.

## HOW IT ACTZ

EACH FIX OV SAEM FING COSTZ 1 MOAR XP (UP 2 10). XP IZ FREE 4 NAO. CLOSIN GIVZ FINGZ BAK. BONK BLOK NEVR BREAKZ.

## PRO TIPZ

IRON GOT 1 HANDMAED SPOT, GOLD AN SHINY GOT 2. STRONGR AN FLOATY NO LIEK EACH OTHR.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH BUK OV HOW 2 MAEK SHOWZ IT ONCE LERND; TEH LIST SHOWZ ALL TEH BETTR FINGZ.
