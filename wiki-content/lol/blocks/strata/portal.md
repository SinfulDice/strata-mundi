## DESCRIPSHUN

A PURPL CURTAIN DAT SWIRLZ SLOWLY AN GLOWZ IN TEH DARK. DIS **PRETEND DOOR 2 NOWHERE** IZ JUS 4 PRETTY: IT GOEZ NOWHERE (4 NAO).

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, BILDIN TAB.

## HOW IT ACTZ

GIVEZ LITE **LEVUL 11**. U WALK THRU IT AN SEE THRU IT. ITZ PIKCHUR **WIGGLEZ**; TEH "WIGGLY PIKCHURS" SETTIN MAEKZ IT STOP.

## PRO TIPZ

MANY DOORZ NEXT 2 EACH OTHR MAEK 1 BIG CURTAIN: TEH SHARED SIDEZ R NOT DRAWN.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

PUT 1 IN A DARK CAEV AN WATCH TEH PURPL SHINY ON TEH ROKZ.
