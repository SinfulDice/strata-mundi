## DESCRIPSHUN

## HOW IT ACTZ

GIVEZ LITE **LEVUL 15**. NOT SOLID: U WALK THRU IT, AN PUTTIN A BLOK THAR REMOOVZ IT. IT SITZ ON A SOLID BLOK OR NEXT 2 A BURNY BLOK, NEVR NEXT 2 WATUR. IT **GETZ OLD**: WIF NUTHIN 2 BURN, IT GOEZ OUT SOON; ELSE IT JUMPZ 2 TEH LOGZ, PLANKZ, LEEFZ, GRAS AN FLOWRZ NEAR, AN NOMZ DEM. TEH "HOT FIRE" PAGE OV TEH HOW STUFF WERKZ TELLZ ALL.

## PRO TIPZ

A BUKKIT OF WATUR NEXT 2 IT = NO MOAR FIRE. 4 A CAMP FIRE DAT NOMZ NUTHIN: TEH WURLD SETTIN `/fire destroy off`.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
