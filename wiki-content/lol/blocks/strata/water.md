## WUT IZ 4

## HOW IT ACTZ

WATUR **FLOWZ**: A **SAUCE** (TEH WATUR OV SEAZ AN LAEKZ) GOEZ DOWN FURST, DEN 2 TEH SIDEZ UP 2 **7 BLOKZ**, TO TEH CLOSEST SLOEP, MOAR AN MOAR THIN. TEH WURLD WATUR NO MOOVZ TIL U BOTHR IT: BREAK A LAEK WALL AN IT FLOWZ. BETWEEN 2 SAUCEZ ON TEH GROUND, NEW SAUCE APPEARZ. TAEK AWAY TEH SAUCE AN TEH FLOW GOEZ AWAY. WATUR TAEKZ PLANTZ AWAY AN MAEKZ HOT JUICE INTO **ROCK** OR **SHINY DARK ROCK**. IT IZ NOT SOLID (NO SWIMMIN YET); U CANT LOOK AT IT OR BREAK IT, BUT U CAN **PUT A BLOK IN ITZ PLAEC**. TEH WURLD OPSHUN `fluids` (`flow: false`) STOPZ TEH FLOW.

## PRO TIPZ

WATUR NO MOOVZ, SO BILD UNDR TEH SEA: PUT WALLZ, BREAK TEH INSIDE, AN U HAZ A DRY BUBBUL. NO WET PAWZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
