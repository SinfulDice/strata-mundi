## DESCRIPSHUN

TEH WATUR OV SEAZ AN LAEKZ AN BIG WATUR, BLU AN SEE-THRU. IT MAEKZ U WANT 2 JUMP IN, WILL HAZ FISHIEZ SUM DAY, AN IZ ALWAYZ PRETTY. KITTEH NO LIEK GETTIN WET THO.

## HOW 2 GETZ

WATUR **HAZ NO ITEM OV ITZ OWN**: IN CREATIF, TEH **BUKKIT OF WATUR** (TOOLZ TAB) PUTZ A SAUCE WIF RITE CLIK, AN TEH EMPTY **BUKKIT** TAEKZ IT BAK. IN TEH WURLD, IT FILLZ ALL TEH LOW PLAECEZ UP 2 WATUR LINE, FROM BIG WATUR 2 TEENY LAEKZ IN FLAT GRAS. IN COLD FLAT LAND, TEH TOP IZ FROZEN.

## WUT IZ 4

PRETTY STUFFZ, NATURAL WALL, LANDMARK: TEH STARTIN PLAEC IZ ALWAYZ NEAR SUM WATUR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

WATUR **FLOWZ**: A **SAUCE** (TEH WATUR OV SEAZ AN LAEKZ) GOEZ DOWN FURST, DEN 2 TEH SIDEZ UP 2 **7 BLOKZ**, TO TEH CLOSEST SLOEP, MOAR AN MOAR THIN. TEH WURLD WATUR NO MOOVZ TIL U BOTHR IT: BREAK A LAEK WALL AN IT FLOWZ. BETWEEN 2 SAUCEZ ON TEH GROUND, NEW SAUCE APPEARZ. TAEK AWAY TEH SAUCE AN TEH FLOW GOEZ AWAY. WATUR TAEKZ PLANTZ AWAY AN MAEKZ HOT JUICE INTO **ROCK** OR **SHINY DARK ROCK**. U **SWIMZ** IN IT: HOLD JUMP 2 GO UP, ELSE U SINK SLOW, AN U GO HALF AS FAST; NEXT 2 A SHORE, A LIL KICK HELPZ U GET OUT. HEAD IN WATUR = ALL BLU. ONLY EMPTY BUKKIT CAN LOOK AT IT AN U CANT BREAK IT, BUT U CAN **PUT A BLOK IN ITZ PLAEC**. TEH TOP LEANZ WIF TEH FLOW AN WIGGLEZ (GRAFIX OPSHUN "WIGGLY WATUR"). TEH WURLD OPSHUN `fluids` (`flow: false`) STOPZ TEH FLOW.

## PRO TIPZ

WATUR NO MOOVZ, SO BILD UNDR TEH SEA: PUT WALLZ, BREAK TEH INSIDE, AN U HAZ A DRY BUBBUL. NO WET PAWZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

U CANT LOOK AT IT, SO ITZ NAEM NEVR SHOWZ AT TEH TOP: U JUS KNOW IT WEN U SEE IT. A NU WURLD ALWAYZ STARTZ U NEAR SUM WATUR.
