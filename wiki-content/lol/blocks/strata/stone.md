## DESCRIPSHUN

ROCK IZ TEH BONEZ OV TEH WURLD: GRAY, STRONK, NEVR LETZ U DOWN. DIG THRU SUM DIRTZ OR SANDZ OR SNOW AN DERE IT IZ, GOIN DOWN 4 A LONG LONG WAY.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV, IT WAITZ IN TEH NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR U TYPE `/give stone`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ TEH INSIDEZ OV EVRY BIOM, UNDR TEH TOP LAYR. ON TEH HIGHEST BIG ROCKZ IT POKEZ OUT IN TEH AIR, DEN SNOW TAKEZ OVR. DEEPR DOWN, IT BECOMEZ DEEP ROCK.

## WUT IZ 4

TEH BILDIN BLOK 4 EVRYTHIN: WALLZ, FLOORZ, TOWRZ DAT TICKLE TEH CLOWDZ. ALSO TEH IN TEH ROCK STRIPEZ LIVE IN IT.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. NO FALLZ, NO MELTZ, NO MOOVZ. VERY CALM. LIEK A NAPPIN KITTEH. DARK HOLEZ WIGGLE THRU IT IN LONG TUNNELZ.

## PRO TIPZ

IF A DARK HOLE OPENZ ON A CLIFF, LOOK DERE 4 IN TEH ROCK STUFFZ. NO NEED 2 DIG ALL TEH THINGZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

LOOK AT IT: ITZ NAEM SHOWZ AT TEH TOP OV TEH SCREEN. WIF F3, TEH "BLOK I SEEZ" LINE SHOWZ ITZ ID 2. MIDDL CLIK PUTZ IT IN TEH BAR.
