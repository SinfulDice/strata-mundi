## DESCRIPSHUN

A HAPPEH PILE OV TEENY PEBBULZ, GRAY AN BEIJ AN BROWN. CRUNCHY ROCKZ NEVR MANAGED 2 BECOME ROCK, BUT IT STAYED HAPPEH.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give gravel`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ TEH FLOOR OV BIG WATUR, AN TEH SHOREZ AN SHALLO WATUR NEXT 2 LOTZ OF TREEZ, BIG ROCKZ AN COLD FLAT LAND.

## WUT IZ 4

GARDN PATHZ, MOUNTIN TRAILZ, RIVR SIDEZ. MAEKZ EVRYTHIN LOOK NATURAL AN A BIT FARMEH.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. LIEK SANDZ, **IT NO FALLZ YET**: UNTIL GRAVITEE COMEZ, IT STAYZ WER U PUT IT, EVEN OVR NOTHIN.

## PRO TIPZ

GO 2 BIG WATUR: TEH FLOOR IZ A GIANT PILE OV CRUNCHY ROCKZ. ALL 4 U.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

LOOK AT IT 2 REED ITZ NAEM AT TEH TOP. UNDR WATUR, TEH LINEZ AROUND TEH BLOK U SEEZ HELP U KNOW CRUNCHY ROCKZ FROM SANDZ.
