## DESCRIPSHUN

SANDZ ROCK IZ SANDZ DAT TOOK A LONG NAP AN GOT HARD. TEH SIDEZ HAZ PRETTY STRIPEZ, TEH TOP IZ SMOOF LIEK A TEMPUL FLOOR.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give sandstone`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ A LAYR OV A FEW BLOKZ RITE UNDR TEH SANDZ OV HOT SANDZ, BEFOR TEH ROCK.

## WUT IZ 4

HOT SANDZ HOUSEZ, PYRAMIDZ, BIG PILLARZ, BATHZ (KITTEH NO LIEK BATHZ). LOOKZ GUD WIF SANDZ AN POINTY TREE PLANKZ.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU, NO MOOVZ. TOP AN BOTTUM R DIFFRENT FROM SIDEZ: STRIPEY WALLZ, PLAIN FLOORZ. NOT LIEK SANDZ, IT WILL NEVR NEED HELP 2 STAY UP.

## PRO TIPZ

DIG A FEW BLOKZ UNDR TEH DUNEZ AN U FINDZ LOTZ OV SANDZ ROCK. NO NEED 2 LOOK MOAR.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

U FINDZ IT WEN U DIG IN HOT SANDZ. LOOK AT IT 2 REED ITZ NAEM AT TEH TOP, OR MIDDL CLIK 2 GRAB IT.
