## WUT IZ 4

## HOW IT ACTZ

## PRO TIPZ

PUT OAK LEEFZ IN A CIRCUL AN U HAZ A SEKRIT GARDN. PERFECT 4 HIDIN UR STUFFZ (AN UR NAPZ).

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
