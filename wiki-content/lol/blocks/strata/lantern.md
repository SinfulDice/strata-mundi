## DESCRIPSHUN

A TINY IRON CAGE WIF A GOLDY HART DAT GLOWZ AN NO WOBBLEZ. STRONGR AN BRITR DAN FIRE STIK. HOUSEZ FEEL ALL COZY.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: BILDIN BLOKZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give lantern`. NOT IN NATURE.

## WUT IZ 4

LITE 4 HOUSEZ, PLAECZ AN BRIJEZ, WEN A FIRE STIK IZ 2 SMOL.

## HOW IT ACTZ

GIVEZ TEH MOST LITE, **LEVUL 15**, 1 LESS EVRY BLOK. IT IZ NOT SOLID AN SITZ **ON A SOLID BLOK**: TAEK DAT BLOK AWAY AN TEH GLOWY BOX FALLZ.

## PRO TIPZ

GLOWY BOX GOEZ A BIT FARR DAN FIRE STIK: GUD IN TEH MIDDL OV A BIG ROOM.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

IT WAITZ 4 U IN TEH BILDIN BLOKZ TAB. LOOK AT IT 2 REED ITZ NAEM AT TEH TOP.
