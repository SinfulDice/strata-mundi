## DESCRIPSHUN

HOT ORANJ ROCK JUICE DAT SLEEPZ AT TEH BOTTOM OV TEH WURLD. IT MAEKZ PRETTY LAEKZ UNDR TEH GROUND… LOOK FROM FAR AWAY PLZ. KITTEH NO LIEK HOT PAWZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

HOT JUICE **HAZ NO ITEM OV ITZ OWN**: IN CREATIF, TEH **BUKKIT OF HOT JUICE** (TOOLZ TAB) PUTZ A SAUCE, AN TEH EMPTY **BUKKIT** TAEKZ IT BAK. IN TEH WURLD, IT FILLZ TEH DARK HOLEZ AN BIG DARK HOLEZ AN BIG CRACKZ WAI DOWN, NEAR TEH BOTTUM ROCK, IN FLAT LAEKZ.

## WUT IZ 4

PRETTY STUFFZ IN TEH DEEP, AN LANDMARK: WEN U SEE IT, TEH BOTTUM ROCK IZ NOT FAR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

HOT JUICE **FLOWZ**, BUT SLOW (1 BLOK EVRY 1.5 SECONDZ) AN NOT FAR: **3 BLOKZ** ON FLAT GROUND, DOWN FURST. TEH WURLD HOT JUICE NO MOOVZ TIL U BOTHR IT. WEN IT TOUCHEZ WATUR, A HOT JUICE SAUCE BECOMEZ **SHINY DARK ROCK** AN FLOWIN HOT JUICE BECOMEZ **ROCK**. IT IZ NOT SOLID: U SINK IN AN **SWIMZ** SUPR SLOW, WAI HARDR DAN WATUR; HEAD IN IT = ALL ORANJ FOG. IT NO BURNZ YET. ONLY EMPTY BUKKIT CAN LOOK AT IT AN U CANT BREAK IT, BUT U CAN **PUT A BLOK IN ITZ PLAEC**.

## PRO TIPZ

2 CROSS A HOT JUICE LAEK, PUT BLOKZ ON TOP AN WALK ON DEM.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

U CANT LOOK AT IT, SO ITZ NAEM NEVR SHOWZ AT TEH TOP: U JUS KNOW IT WEN U GO WAI DOWN IN TEH DARK HOLEZ.
