RITE CLIK WIF DIRT SCRATCHR ON DIRTZ OR GRAS BLOK WIF NOTHIN ON TOP. BREAK IT, U GETZ DIRTZ BAK.

## WUT IZ 4

FOODZ WILL GROW ON IT LATR. 4 NAO IT MAKEZ PRETTY GARDENZ.

## HOW IT ACTZ

NO BURN, NO FALL. DIRT SCRATCHR NO WERK IF SUMTHIN IZ ON TOP.

## PRO TIPZ

MAEK LINEZ OV IT BETWEEN BUSHEZ 4 UR FUTURE FARMZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH DIRT SCRATCHR TIP SEZ RITE CLIK FIXEZ UP TEH BLOK.
