## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give birch_log`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ TEH TRUNK OV WHITE TREEZ IN **LOTZ OF TREEZ**, WIF TEH OAKZ (AN SOON IN WHITE TREE FORESTZ): TALL AN SKINNY, SMOL ROUND HAT.

## WUT IZ 4

BEEMZ, PILLARZ, ROOF STUFFZ AN CABINZ, IN A NEW COLOR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. LIEK ALL LOGZ, IT **TURNZ**: ON TOP OR BOTTUM IT STANDZ UP, ON TEH SIDE IT LIEZ DOWN TEH WAY U CLIKD.

## PRO TIPZ

MIX IT WIF OTHR WOODZ 4 PRETTY WALLZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
