## DESCRIPSHUN

A WOOD STIK WIF A TINY FIRE ON TOP. EVRY XPLORR KITTEH NEEDZ IT: WER IT BURNZ, DARK AN CAVEZ GO AWAY.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: BILDIN BLOKZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give torch`. NOT IN NATURE.

## WUT IZ 4

MAEK CAVEZ, MINE HOLEZ, HOUSEZ AN PATHZ NOT DARK. LEEV SUM BEHIND U 2 FIND TEH WAY OUT.

## HOW IT ACTZ

GIVEZ LITE **LEVUL 14** (OV 15), 1 LESS EVRY BLOK, AN IT GOEZ AROUND STUFF. IT GOEZ THRU AIR, WATUR AN SEE-THRU BLOK, BUT NOT ROCK. IT IZ NOT SOLID. IT SITZ STRAIT **ON A SOLID BLOK** OR LEENZ **ON A WALL**, DEPENDZ WICH SIDE U LOOK AT; UNDR A ROOF IT FINDZ TEH FLOOR OR A WALL NEARBY. TAEK TEH BLOK DAT HOLDZ IT AN TEH FIRE STIK FALLZ.

## PRO TIPZ

1 FIRE STIK EVRY 10-12 BLOKZ = NICE BRITE HALLWAY. ON TEH EDGE OV A BLOK, ITZ LITE GOEZ IN TEH NEXT COLUM 2.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

IT WAITZ 4 U IN TEH BILDIN BLOKZ TAB. LOOK AT IT 2 REED ITZ NAEM AT TEH TOP.
