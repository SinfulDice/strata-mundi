## DESCRIPSHUN

A TUFF WOOD TABUL WIF A GRID ON TOP, A SAW AN A HAMMR ON TEH SIDE. WER IDEAZ BECOM STUFFZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

4 PLANKZ OV ANY TREE IN A SQUARE IN TEH 2×2 GRID OV TEH STUFF BOX. U KNOWZ IT FROM TEH START. IN CRAYTIV: BILDIN TAB, OR `/give crafting_table`.

## WUT IZ 4

RITE CLIK OPENZ A **3×3 GRID**: ALL TEH BIG HOW 2 MAEK (TREZUR BOX, BARRUL, FLOATEEZ…) GO HERE. TEH "MAH DIARY" BUTTON OPENZ TEH BUK OF HOW 2 MAEK, IT PUTZ TEH STUFFZ 4 U.

## HOW IT ACTZ

CLOSE TEH SCREEN AN TEH STUFFZ IN TEH GRID GO BAK IN UR STUFF BOX (OR ON TEH GROUND IF FULL). WALK AWAI OR BREAK TEH TABUL: SAME.

## PRO TIPZ

PUT STUFFZ AT RANDUM, NO PROBLEM: IF IT MAEKZ SUMTHIN, IT WERKZ AN GOEZ IN TEH BUK. A TINY MESSUJ SEZ WEN STUFFZ LIEK EACH OTHR.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH BUK OF HOW 2 MAEK (MAH DIARY KEY OR BUTTON) SHOWZ IT FROM TEH START.
