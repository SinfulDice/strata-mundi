## DESCRIPSHUN

ROUND IRON BLAYD ON A ROCK: CHOPZ A BLOK IN2 OTHR LOOKZ, NO BURNY STUFF.

## HOW 2 GETZ

1 IRON BAR OVR 3 ROCKZ AT TEH CRAFTIN TABUL. DIG IT WIF DIGGY STIK. IN CRAYTIV: WERKIN BLOKZ TAB.

## WUT IZ 4

RITE CLIK: PUT BLOK IN LEFT SPOT, PIK A LOOK IN TEH LIST (ROCK → ROCK BRIKZ OR SMOOF ROCK, SANDZ ROCK → CHOPPD SANDZ ROCK), DEN TAEK IT. SHIFT-CLIK CHOPZ TEH WHOLE STAK.

## HOW IT ACTZ

EACH CHOP USEZ 1 BLOK. CLOSIN TEH SCREEN GIVZ TEH BLOK BAK.

## PRO TIPZ

EZIER DAN CRAFTIN TABUL: 1 ROCK = 1 BRIK, NOTHIN LOST. MOAR BLOK FAMLYZ (SLABZ, STAIRZ…) COMIN.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
