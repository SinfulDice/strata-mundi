## DESCRIPSHUN

ROUND ROCKZ STUK IN GOOP. WUT ROCK LOOKZ LIEK AFTR A DIGGY STIK. LESS SMOOF, SAME TUFF.

## HOW 2 GETZ

BREAK ROCK WIF A DIGGY STIK AN U GETZ LUMPY ROCK. IN CRAYTIV: BILDIN TAB, OR `/give cobblestone`.

## WUT IZ 4

TUFF BLOK, LOTZ OV IT. SOON IT MAEKZ TEH FURST HOT BOXEZ.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. NEEDZ A DIGGY STIK 2 KEEP IT.

## PRO TIPZ

MINEZ GIV LOTZ OV LUMPY ROCK: KEEP A BOX OV IT NEER TEH DOOR.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

BREAK ROCK WIF A DIGGY STIK: IT FALLZ LIEK DIS.
