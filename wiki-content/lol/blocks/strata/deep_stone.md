## WUT IZ 4

DARK BILDINZ, BASEMENT FLOORZ, MAKIN PRETTY STRIPEZ WIF NORMAL ROCK. ALSO TEH DEEP IN TEH ROCK STUFFZ LIVE IN IT.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU, NO MOOVZ. IT IZ HARDR DAN ROCK. IN CRAYTIV NOBODY CAREZ (ONE CLIK, GONE), BUT IT WILL MATTR WEN SURVIVL COMEZ.

## PRO TIPZ

SHINY ONLY HIDEZ AT TEH VERY BOTTUM. WEN ROCK TURNZ IN2 DEEP ROCK, U IZ GETTIN WARM.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
