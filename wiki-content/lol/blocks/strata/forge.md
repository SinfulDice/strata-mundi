## DESCRIPSHUN

ROCK FIERPLAES WIF HOT COALZ IN IRON RING: HERE BARZ BECOM CLINKY LOOPZ, ARMR PLAETZ AN JUMPY BOOTZ, AN SHINY GETZ CUT IN SKALEZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

3 IRON BARZ ON TOP OV HOT BOX, 5 ROCKZ AROUND, AT TEH CRAFTIN TABUL. BREAK WIF PICKY. IN CRAYTIV: WERKIN BLOKZ TAB.

## WUT IZ 4

RITE CLIK 2 OPEN. LEFT: LIST OV ALL IT MAEKZ ("<" ">" OR WHEEL WEN 2 LONG). RITE: FIER AN 4 SPOTZ, DEN TEH RESULT. PIK: STUFFZ JUMP IN, DEN TAEK IT.

## HOW IT ACTZ

IT MELTZ ROCKZ 2: **4 ROCKZ + 1 COAL = 5 BARZ**, NAO. EVRY METUL PIECE **EATZ 1 COAL**. SHINY SKALEZ NO NEED FIER. CLOSIN GIVZ STUFFZ BAK.

## PRO TIPZ

2 BARZ + 1 COAL = 4 LOOPZ: MAEK LOTZ AT ONCE.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
