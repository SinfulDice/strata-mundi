## DESCRIPSHUN

A SUPR HOT BOX SHINY: IT ONLY COOKZ **ROCK STUFFZ**, 2 TIEMZ FASTR DAN HOT BOX. ITS FACE LOOKZ AT U WEN U PUT IT AN GLOWZ WHILE TEH FIRE BURNZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

RITE THER, WIF A **SHINY HOT BOX KIT** (RITE CLIK) ON A GOLD ONE; OR AT TEH CRAFTIN TABUL, TEH GOLD ONE WIF 4 SHINIEZ AN 4 SEE-THRU BLOKZ AROUND. DIG IT WIF DIGGY STIK. IN CRAYTIV: WERKIN BLOKZ TAB.

## WUT IZ 4

RITE CLIK: STUFF 2 COOK ON TOP, BURNY STUFF BELOW (COAL, WOOD COAL, WOOD, HOT JUICE BUKKIT…), RESULT ON TEH RITE. 1 COOK TAKEZ **1.5 SEKONDZ** AN 1 COAL COOKZ **16**. UPGRAYD SPOTZ: **3**.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FIRE ONLY STARTZ IF THER IZ STUFF 2 COOK AN ROOM 4 TEH RESULT; FULL RESULT STOPZ EVRYTHIN. IT KEEPZ COOKIN WIF SCREEN CLOSD, IF ITS PLACE IZ LOADED. BROKN, IT DROPZ ALL STUFFZ.

## PRO TIPZ

CHIPZ (ZOOM, LES BURNY, DUBBL STUFF) GO IN UPGRAYD SPOTZ; 2 SAME CHIPZ NO ADD UP. SHIFT-CLIK PUTZ STUFF IN TEH RITE SPOT.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH BUK OF HOW 2 MAEK IN MAH DIARY SHOWZ HOW, ONCE U LERND IT.
