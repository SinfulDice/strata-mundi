## DESCRIPSHUN

A NICE OAK TRUNK WIF RUFF BROWN BARK AN CIRCLEZ AT TEH ENDZ. IT SMELLZ LIEK FOREST AN TREE HOUSEZ. ALSO GR8 4 SCRATCHIN.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give oak_log`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ TEH TRUNK OV OAK TREEZ: A FEW IN FLAT GRAS, LOTZ IN LOTZ OF TREEZ. OAKZ ONLY GROW ON GRAS BLOKZ.

## WUT IZ 4

BEEMZ, PILLARZ, ROOF STUFFZ, WINDOW FRAEMZ, CABINZ. LYIN DOWN, IT MAEKZ GR8 BENCHEZ 4 NAPPIN.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. TEH LOG **TURNZ**: PUT ON TEH TOP OR BOTTUM OV A BLOK, IT STANDZ UP. PUT ON TEH SIDE OV A BLOK, IT LIEZ DOWN THE WAY OV TEH SIDE U CLIKD, BARK AN LINEZ ALL STRAIT. LYIN LOGZ HAZ NO OWN ITEM: SAEM LOG, SAEM NAEM.

## PRO TIPZ

4 A NICE ROOF, CLIK TEH SIDE DAT POINTZ WER U WANT. DAT SIDE DECIDEZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

LOOK AT A TRUNK: ITZ NAEM SHOWZ AT TEH TOP. F3 ALSO SHOWZ TEH EGZAKT ID OV TEH BLOK, EVEN TEH LYIN ONE. MIDDL CLIK ON A TREE GIVEZ U A COPY.
