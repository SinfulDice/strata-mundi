## DESCRIPSHUN

BOTTUM ROCK IZ TEH FLOOR OV TEH WURLD. BUMPY, DARK, STUBBORN LAYR AT TEH VERY BOTTUM SO NOTHIN FALLZ OUT. LIEK A KITTEH WHO WONT MOOV OFF TEH KEYBORD.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give bedrock`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ AT TEH BOTTUM OV EVRY COLUMN: ONE FULL ROW, PLUS SUM RANDUM BLOKZ ON TOP SO IT IZ BUMPY. DARK HOLEZ NEVR DIG IN2 IT.

## WUT IZ 4

IN CRAYTIV, A FANCEH DECORASHUN BLOK. IN SURVIVL, IT WILL B TEH END: NOBODY GOEZ UNDR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU, NO MOOVZ. **NO BREAKIN IF U IZ NOT IN CRAYTIV.** IN CRAYTIV, UR SUPR CLIK BREAKZ IT LIEK ANYTHIN, BUT PLZ NOT 2 MUCH. IT IZ DERE 4 A REESON.

## PRO TIPZ

IF U SEE BOTTUM ROCK WEN DIGGIN, U IZ AT TEH BOTTUM. GO UP A BIT: DAT IZ WER SHINY HIDEZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

LOOK AT IT 2 REED ITZ NAEM AT TEH TOP. F3 SHOWZ HOW HIGH U IZ: WEN TEH NUMBR IZ TEENY, BOTTUM ROCK IZ CLOSE.
