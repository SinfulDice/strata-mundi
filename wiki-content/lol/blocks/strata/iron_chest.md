## DESCRIPSHUN

## HOW 2 GETZ

CREATIV: STASH TAB. SURVIVL: KIT ON OLD TREZUR BOX, STUFF STAYZ INSIDE. OR CRAFT IT.

## WUT 4

RITE CLIK OPENZ. ROWZ SCROLL. UPGRAYDZ GO IN SIDE PANUL: TIDY, PICKY, SQUISH, NOM VOID, GIMME.

## HOW IT WERKZ

STUFF AN UPGRAYDZ SAVED. BREAK IT: EVRYTHIN FALLZ OUT.

## TIPZ

GIMME BIT + PICKY BIT = BOX DAT CLEENZ UP BY ITSELF.
