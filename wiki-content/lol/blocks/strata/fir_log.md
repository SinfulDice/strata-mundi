## DESCRIPSHUN

A POINTY TREE TRUNK, DARKR AN SKINNIER DAN OAK, WIF DEEP LINEZ IN TEH BARK. IT LIVED ALL ITZ LIFE IN TEH COLD AN U CAN TELL: DIS LOG HAZ ATTITUDE.

## HOW 2 GETZ

IN CRAYTIV: NATURE STUFFZ TAB OV TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX, OR `/give fir_log`. IN TEH WURLD, IT IZ TEH TRUNK OV POINTY TREEZ, ON TEH SIDEZ OV BIG ROCKZ AN, LESS OFTEN, IN COLD FLAT LAND. DEY GROW ON GRAS BLOKZ, SNOWY OR NOT.

## WUT IZ 4

MOUNTIN HOUSEZ, COZY CABINZ, DARK BEEMZ DAT LOOK GUD WIF LITE PLANKZ. LYIN DOWN, IT MAEKZ NICE RAILZ.

## HOW IT ACTZ

FULL BLOK, NO SEE-THRU. LIEK ALL LOGZ, IT **TURNZ**: STANDZ UP ON TEH TOP OR BOTTUM OV A BLOK, LIEZ DOWN TEH WAY OV TEH SIDE U CLIKD. LYIN LOGZ KEEP TEH SAEM NAEM AN HAZ NO OWN ITEM.

## PRO TIPZ

POINTY TREEZ R TALLR DAN OAKZ. MIDDL CLIK ON TEH TRUNK IZ FASTR DAN LOOKIN IN TEH MAGIC STUFF BOX.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT
