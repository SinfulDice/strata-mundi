## DESCRIPSHUN

`/fluids` MAEKZ TEH WATR AN LAVA GO SPLOOSH OR STOP, AN SETZ HOW MANY WET BLOKZ TEH SERVR UPDAETZ EACH TICK. TEH SETTIN IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD; U CAN ALSO PICK IT WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD (TEH "WATR AN LAVA" BUTTN).

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/fluids [on|off|query|<budget>]`
- `on`: WATR GOEZ SPLOOSH.
- `off`: WATR STAYZ STUK, RITE WER IT IZ.
- `query` (OR NUTHIN): SHOWZ TEH SETTIN NAO.
- `<budget>`: MAX WATR UPDAETZ PER TICK (2048 IF U NO SAY); TEH REST WAITZ 4 NEXT TICK.

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/fluids off`: FREEZEZ A LAEK WHILE U BILD.
- `/fluids 512`: SLOWR SPLOOSH BUT CHEEPR, GUD 4 TINY COMPUTR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. STOPPIN TEH WATR FORGETZ TEH BLOKZ DAT WUZ FLOWIN: DEY ONLY GO AGAIN WEN A BLOK NEXT 2 DEM CHANGEZ AFTR `/fluids on`.

## PRO TIPZ

A SMOLR BUDGIT NO CHANGEZ HOW TEH WATR ENDZ UP, ONLY HOW FAST IT GETZ THAR WEN LOTZ OV WATR MOOVZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

`/help fluids` SHOWZ HOW 2 TYPE IT; TAB FINISHEZ `on`, `off` AN `query`.
