## DESCRIPSHUN

`/tp` ZOOPZ U RITE AWAY 2 EGZAKT NUMBRZ. GR8 4 EXPLORIN, FINDIN UR BILDIN AGAIN, OR VISITIN A BIG ROCK TOP U SAW FAR AWAY.

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/tp <x> <y> <z>`. EACH NUMBR CAN B:
- A NUMBR (WIF DOTZ OK);
- `~`: WER U R NAO;
- `~` AN A NUMBR: THAT MUCH FROM WER U R (`~5`, `~-10`).

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/tp 0 200 0`: GO 2 TEH MIDDL OV TEH WURLD, HIGH ABOVE TEH GROUND.
- `/tp ~ ~20 ~`: GO UP TWENTY BLOKZ RITE HERE.
- `/tp ~100 ~ ~`: LIL HOP 2 TEH EEST.

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. U ARRIVE **FLYIN** AN NOT MOOVIN: NO SURPRIZ FALL. IF U ASK 2 GO 2 FAR, TEH SERVR PUTZ U BAK INSIDE TEH WURLD EDGE AN TEH WURLD HEIGHT. TEH CONSOLE SAYZ WER U REELY LANDED.

## PRO TIPZ

WRITE DOWN TEH NUMBRZ FROM F3 BEFOR U GO: ONE `/tp` AN U IZ BAK HOEM. LIEK A KITTEH WHO ALWAYZ FINDZ DINNR.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

`/help tp` SHOWZ HOW 2 TYPE IT, AN TAB FINISHEZ TEH COMMAND NAEM. F3 SHOWZ WER U R 2 DO TEH MATHZ.
