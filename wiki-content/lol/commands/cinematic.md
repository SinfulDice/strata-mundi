## DESCRIPSHUN

`/cinematic` PLAYZ A MOOVEE: A CAMRA TRIP WRITTEN IN A PAK, DAT FLIEZ AROUND LIEK IN A FILM. TEH BASIC PAK HAZ ONE 4 SHOWIN OFF. POPCORN NOT INCLUDED.

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/cinematic <id>`, WER `id` IZ TEH FULL ID OV TEH MOOVEE (WIF NAEMSPACE).

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/cinematic strata:showcase`: TEH CAMRA GOEZ UP AN SPINZ AROUND WER U R.

## HOW IT ACTZ

UR SCREEN DOEZ IT ALL ALONE, IN EVRY GAEM MOAD. TEH MOOVEE STARTZ FROM **WER U R**: SAEM MOOVEE WORKZ EVRYWER. WHILE IT PLAYZ, U NO MOOV, TEH BUTTONZ GO AWAY 4 BLAK BARZ AN MAYBE WORDZ AT TEH BOTTUM, DEN U SEE FROM UR EYEZ AGAIN. ESC (OR B ON TEH GAEM PAD) SKIPZ IT IF TEH MOOVEE SAYZ OK. WRONG ID? TEH CONSOLE SAYZ NO SUCH MOOVEE.

## PRO TIPZ

STAND IN AN OPEN PLAEC BEFOR U PLAI A MOOVEE: TEH CAMRA NO GOEZ AROUND STUFFZ.
