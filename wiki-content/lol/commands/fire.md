## DESCRIPSHUN

`/fire` SETZ HOW HOT FIRE ACTZ IN DIS WURLD. TEH SETTIN IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD; U CAN ALSO PICK IT WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD (TEH "HOT FIRE" AN "BURNY" BUTTNZ).

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/fire spread on|off`, `/fire destroy on|off`, `/fire query`
- `spread off`: HOT FIRE NO MAEK NEW FIREZ; IT GETZ OLD AN GOEZ OUT WER IT IZ.
- `destroy off`: BURNY STUFF IZ NOT NOMMED (FIRE STILL GOEZ OUT IN TEH END).
- `query` (OR NUTHIN): SHOWZ BOTH SETTINZ.

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/fire spread off`: A FIREPLAYCE DAT NEVR BURNZ TEH HOUSE.
- `/fire destroy off` DEN `/fire spread on`: A BIG SHOWY FIRE DAT LEEVZ NUTHIN BEHIND.

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. FIREZ ALREDDY BURNIN FOLLOW TEH NEW SETTIN RITE AWAY.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

`/help fire` SHOWZ HOW 2 TYPE IT; TAB FINISHEZ `spread`, `destroy`, `query`, DEN `on` AN `off`.
