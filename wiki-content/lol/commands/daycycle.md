## DESCRIPSHUN

`/daycycle` MAEKZ TEH TIEM GO ON OR STOP. TEH SETTIN IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD; U CAN ALSO PICK IT WEN U MAEK TEH WURLD (TEH "DAI AN NITE" BUTTN).

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/daycycle [on|off|query]`
- `on`: TIEM GOEZ ON, SUN AN MUN MOOV.
- `off`: TIEM STAYZ STUK (GUD WIF `/time set` 2 BILD IN SUNNY DAI 4EVR).
- `query` (OR NUTHIN): SHOWZ TEH SETTIN NAO.

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/time set noon` DEN `/daycycle off`: NOON 4EVR.
- `/daycycle on`: TEH DAI GOEZ ON AGAIN.

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. TIEM ALSO STOPZ WEN TEH GAEM IZ PAWSD.

## PRO TIPZ

`/time add` WERKZ EVEN WEN TIEM IZ STUK: SKIP SUM HOURZ WIFOUT STARTIN TEH CYCL AGAIN.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

`/help daycycle` SHOWZ HOW 2 TYPE IT; TAB FINISHEZ `on`, `off` AN `query`.
