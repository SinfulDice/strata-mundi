## DESCRIPSHUN

`/time` REEDZ OR SETZ TEH WURLD CLOK. EACH WURLD HAZ ITZ OWN TIEM, IT GOEZ 4WARD ALL ALONE WHILE U PLAI (NOT WEN PAWSD OR WIF `/daycycle off`) AN IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD. TEH SKY FOLLOWZ IT: SUN, MUN, SHINY DOTZ AN DAYLITE.

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/time <set|add|query> [value]`
- `query` (OR NUTHIN): SHOWZ TEH TIEM NAO AN WICH DAI IT IZ.
- `set <value>`: GOEZ 2 TEH NEXT TIEM U ASK, WIF A WORD (`day` RITE AFTR SUN COMIN UP, `noon` 4 MIDDL OV DAY, `night` RITE AFTR SUN GOIN DOWN, `midnight`) OR A NUMBR OV TICKZ AFTR SUN COMIN UP.
- `add <ticks>`: MOOVZ TEH CLOK 4WARD DAT MANY TICKZ.

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/time set noon`: SUNBATH TIEM.
- `/time set night`: NITE COMEZ, MUN SAYZ HAI.
- `/time add 24000`: 1 DAI LATR, SAME TIEM.

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. A DAI ON TERRA IZ 24000 TICKZ (20 MINUTZ): HALF DAI, HALF NITE. TIEM NEVR GOEZ BAK: `set` GOEZ 2 TEH NEXT TIEM U ASK, 2MORROW IF IT ALREDDY PASSD. TEH MUN CHANGEZ SHAEP EVRY NITE, 8 NITEZ IN A CYCL.

## PRO TIPZ

TEH MUN SHAEPZ COUNT TEH DAIZ: 8 NITEZ FROM 1 ROUND MUN 2 TEH NEXT.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

`/help time` SHOWZ HOW 2 TYPE IT AN TEH WORDZ; TAB FINISHEZ `set`, `add`, `query` AN TEH TIEMZ.
