## DESCRIPSHUN

`/time` REEDZ OR SETZ TEH WURLD CLOK. EACH WURLD HAZ ITZ OWN TIEM, IT GOEZ 4WARD ALL ALONE WHILE U PLAI AN IZ SAEVD WIF TEH WURLD.

## HOW 2 TYPE

`/time <set|add|query> [value]`
- `query`: SHOWZ TEH TIEM NAO.
- `set <value>`: SETZ TEH TIEM, WIF A WORD (`day` 4 SUN COMIN UP, `noon` 4 MIDDL OV DAY, `night` 4 SUN GOIN DOWN, `midnight`) OR A NUMBR OV TICKZ.
- `add <ticks>`: MOOVZ TEH CLOK 4WARD DAT MANY TICKZ.

## EGZAMPLZ

- `/time set noon`: SUNBATH TIEM.
- `/time query`: IZ IT DINNR TIEM YET?

## HOW IT ACTZ

TEH SERVR DOEZ IT, **CRAYTIV ONLY**. TEH ANSWR GIVEZ TEH TIEM IN TICKZ, OUT OV A WHOLE DAY; AFTR DAT, TEH CLOK JUS STARTZ AGAIN FROM ZERO. IN V0.1, TIEM IZ COUNTED AN SAEVD, BUT **TEH SKY NO CHANGEZ YET**: DAY AN NITE U CAN SEE COMEZ LATR.

## PRO TIPZ

TEH WORDZ R A QUARTR OV A DAY APART: GUD 2 KNOW WER U R WIFOUT MATHZ.
