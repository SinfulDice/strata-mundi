## DESCRIPSHUN

WATUR, WATUR, AN MOAR WATUR. BIG WATUR GOEZ FAR AWAY FROM TEH LANDZ, DEEP AN BLU, WIF SUM LIL SANDZ ISLANDZ POKIN OUT. KITTEH SAYZ NOPE.

## HOW 2 GETZ

BIG WATUR IZ IN TEH PLAECEZ FARRRST FROM TEH MIDDL OV TEH LANDZ, WER TEH GROUND GOEZ WAY UNDR WATUR LINE.

## WUT IZ 4

LONG TRIPZ, UNDR-WATUR BASEZ, LITE HOUSEZ ON LIL SANDZ ISLANDZ, AN SO MUCH CRUNCHY ROCKZ ON TEH FLOOR.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **WATUR**: NO MOOVZ, UP 2 WATUR LINE. NO FLOWZ, U GO THRU LIEK AIR.
- **FLOOR**: A LAYR OV CRUNCHY ROCKZ, DEN ROCK.
- **LANDZ ABOVE WATUR**: SANDZ, ON TOP AN UNDR.
- **PLANTZ**: NONE, 4 NAO.
- DARK HOLEZ NEVR OPEN IN TEH SEA FLOOR: DEY STAY UNDR A ROCK ROOF, NICE AN DRY.

## PRO TIPZ

WATUR NO MOOVZ, SO: PUT BLOKZ WER TEH WATUR IZ, DEN BREAK DEM, AN U GETZ DRY UNDR-WATUR ROOMZ. NO WET PAWZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

NO BIOM NAEM ON SCREEN YET: U KNOW BIG WATUR CUZ IT IZ WATUR 2 TEH END OV TEH WURLD. FLY OVR IT, OR CHECK TEH FLOOR WIF F3 2 GESS HOW DEEP IT IZ.
