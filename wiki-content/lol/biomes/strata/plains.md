## DESCRIPSHUN

BIG BIG FIELDZ OV SOFT GRAS AS FAR AS U CAN SEE, WIF LONELY OAK TREEZ. FLAT GRAS IZ TEH BIOM 4 PIKNIKZ, LONG WALKZ AN FURST HOUSEZ. AN ZOOMIEZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

FLAT GRAS SHOWZ IN NOT-2-HOT, KINDA DRY PLAECEZ, ON SOFT GROUND. IT IZ ONE OV TEH 2 BIOMZ WER TEH WURLD CAN **START** U (WIF LOTZ OF TREEZ).

## WUT IZ 4

BEST GROUND 4 BILDIN: FLAT, OPEN, WIF JUS ENUF TREEZ 4 PRETTY.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **GROUND**: GRAS BLOKZ, DIRTZ UNDR, DEN ROCK.
- **PLANTZ**: A FEW OAK TREEZ HERE AN DERE.
- **SHOREZ**: SANDZ, ON TEH BEACHEZ AN UNDR TEH SHALLO WATUR.
- TEH EDJEZ WIF OTHR BIOMZ R SOFT: TOP BLOKZ MIX A BIT DERE.

## PRO TIPZ

BILD UR FURST BASE HERE: U SEE FAR, AN WATUR IZ NEVR FAR FROM TEH START.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH GAEM NO SHOWZ BIOM NAEMZ YET: U KNOW FLAT GRAS BY TEH SHORT GRAS AN LONELY OAKZ. F3 GIVEZ WER U IZ, GUD 2 REMEMBR UR FAVRIT FLAT GRAS.
