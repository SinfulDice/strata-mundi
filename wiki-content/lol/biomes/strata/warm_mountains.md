## DESCRIPSHUN

MOUNTINZ IN HOT PLAECEZ: GRAZZY SLOPEZ WIF FLAT TREEZ, DEN RED SANDZ AN BARE ROCKZ AT TEH TOP. DEY SIT BETWEEN COLD MOUNTINZ AN STRIPY HILLZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

TOASTY MOUNTINZ GROWZ INLAND, WERE TEH GROUND IZ YUNG AN POINTY, IN HOT, KINDA DRY PLAECEZ.

## WUT IZ 4

FLAT TREE WOODZ UP HIGH, RED SANDZ AN STONEZ, AN PRETTY VIEWZ OV TEH STRIPY HILLZ.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **GROUND**: GRAZ ON LOW SLOPEZ, DIRTZ UNDR.
- **HIGHNESS**: ABOUT 165 BLOKZ, GRAZ TURNZ IN2 **RED SANDZ**, DEN ABOUT 200 BLOKZ, **BARE STONEZ**; MOAR HIGH (ABOUT 230 BLOKZ), **SNOWY POINTZ**.
- **PLANTZ**: FLAT TREEZ, TALL GRAZ AN BUSHEZ ON TEH GRAZ, DED BUSHEZ ON TEH RED SANDZ.
- **SHOREZ**: GRAVULZ NEXT 2 WATR.

## PRO TIPZ

RED SANDZ IZ WERE TREEZ STOP: ABOVE IT, NO MOAR FLAT TREEZ 2 CHOP.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

NO BIOM NAEM ON SCREEN YET: FLAT TREEZ UP HIGH AN RED SANDZ TELLZ U.
