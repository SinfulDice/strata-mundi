## DESCRIPSHUN

LOTZ AN LOTZ OV OAK TREEZ, SUPR GREEN. TEH TREE HEADZ ALMOST TOUCH AN U SNEAK BETWEEN TRUNKZ. IT SMELLZ LIEK MOSS, BARK AN ADVENCHUR. KITTEH HEAVEN.

## HOW 2 GETZ

LOTZ OF TREEZ SHOWZ IN NOT-2-HOT, WET PLAECEZ, ON SOFT GROUND. WIF FLAT GRAS, IT IZ ONE OV TEH 2 BIOMZ WER TEH WURLD CAN **START** U.

## WUT IZ 4

INFINIT OAK LOGZ AN LEEFZ, AN TEH PERFECT PLAEC 4 A TREE HOUSE OR A FAIRY VILLAJ… DAT U MAEK UP.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **GROUND**: GRAS BLOKZ, DIRTZ UNDR, DEN ROCK.
- **PLANTZ**: LOTZ OV OAKZ, ALL SQUISHED 2GETHR.
- **SHOREZ**: CRUNCHY ROCKZ ON TEH SIDEZ AN UNDR TEH SHALLO WATUR.
- GOIN 2 FLAT GRAS IZ SOFT: TEH TREEZ GET FEWR AN FEWR.

## PRO TIPZ

FLY ABOVE TEH TREEZ 2 KNOW WER U IZ: FROM UP DERE, LOTZ OF TREEZ IZ A GREEN CARPIT, EZ 2 TELL FROM FLAT GRAS.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

NO BIOM NAEM ON SCREEN YET: ALL TEH OAKZ R ENUF 2 KNOW IT. LOOK AT A TREE 2 REED TEH NAEM OV ITZ BLOKZ AT TEH TOP.
