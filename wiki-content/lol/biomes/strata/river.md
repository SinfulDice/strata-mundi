## DESCRIPSHUN

A LONG LONG RIBBON OV CALM WATUR GOIN WIGGLY BETWEEN TEH HILLZ, WIF PALE SANDZ ON TEH SIDEZ. WIGGLY WATUR GOEZ THRU OTHR BIOMZ AN NO ASKZ PERMISHUN. LIEK A KITTEH. IT TIEZ TEH LANDZ 2GETHR LIEK A BLU STRING. KITTEH WANTZ 2 PLAY WIF STRING.

## HOW 2 GETZ

WIGGLY WATUR SHOWZ WERVR TEH GROUND IZ LOW ENUF, HOT OR COLD NO MATTR: IT GOEZ IN LONG WIGGLY LINEZ ACROS TEH LANDZ. TEH WIGGLY WATUR BIOM IZ ONLY TEH BOTTUM, UNDR TEH WATUR; TEH SIDEZ R OTHR BIOMZ. WHEN TEH GROUND GOEZ UP, WIGGLY WATUR GOEZ AWAY AN LEAVEZ ONLY A DRY VALLY IN TEH HILL.

## WUT IZ 4

A NATURE ROAD 2 EXPLOR AN NO GET LOST, WATUR ALWAYZ CLOSE, LOTZ OV SANDZ, AN A FREE MOAT 2 KEEP UR HOUSE SAEF.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **WATUR**: NO MOOVZ, UP 2 WATUR LINE, LIEK BIG WATUR. NO FLOWZ (NO SWIMMIN AGENST TEH WATUR YET).
- **BOTTUM**: SANDZ, ALL ACROS.
- **SIDEZ**: SOFT SLOPEZ, NO CLIFFZ, NO ROCK ROOFZ: U CAN ALWAYZ WALK OUT. NO WET KITTEH STUCK.
- **PLANTZ**: NONE IN TEH WATUR.
- **HOW HIGH**: MOAR HIGH GROUND, MOAR SKINNY WIGGLY WATUR, TIL ONLY A DRY VALLY IZ LEFT.
- EVEN IN COLD PLAECEZ, WIGGLY WATUR NO GETZ FROZEN.

## PRO TIPZ

LOST? FOLLOW WIGGLY WATUR: IT GOEZ WIGGLY THRU TEH LOW LANDZ, ALWAYZ AT WATUR LINE, AN SEEZ LOTZ OV PLAECEZ. WER IT TURNZ IN2 A DRY VALLY, DAT IZ A GUD WAY 2 GO UP TEH HILLZ AN NO CLIMB.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

TEH GAEM NO SHOWZ BIOM NAEMZ YET: U KNOW WIGGLY WATUR BY TEH SKINNY WIGGLY WATUR STRIPE WIF SANDZ ON TEH BOTTUM. F3 SHOWZ HOW HIGH U R: NEXT 2 TEH WATUR, U R AT WATUR LINE.
