## DESCRIPSHUN

SUPR HIGH POINTY TOPZ, DEEP VALLEEZ AN SNOWY TOPZ DAT SCRATCH TEH SKY. BIG ROCKZ IZ TEH BIOM 4 WOW VIEWZ AN BIG CRAZY PROJEKTZ. KITTEH LIEKS HIGH PLAECEZ.

## HOW 2 GETZ

BIG ROCKZ POP UP IN TEH MIDDL OV TEH LANDZ, WER TEH GROUND IZ YUNG AN NOT WORN DOWN. DEY MAEK SHARP EDJEZ WIF WIDE VALLEEZ BETWEEN, AN DEY R TEH HIGHEST STUFFZ IN TEH WURLD BY FAR.

## WUT IZ 4

LOOKOUTZ, CASTLEZ ON CLIFFZ, TUNNELZ IN TEH ROCK, AN ROCK CLIFFZ WER IN TEH ROCK STUFFZ SUMTIMEZ SHOW.

## HOW IT ACTZ

- **GROUND**: GRAS BLOKZ ON TEH SLOPEZ, DIRTZ UNDR.
- **HOW HIGH**: ABOVE SUM HEIGHT, GRAS BECOMEZ NAKED ROCK, AN EVEN HIGHR, SNOW BLOK HATZ.
- **PLANTZ**: POINTY TREEZ ON TEH GRAS SLOPEZ.
- **SHOREZ**: CRUNCHY ROCKZ NEXT 2 WATUR.

## PRO TIPZ

FLY 2 TEH TOP AN LOOK AROUND: BEST PLAEC 2 SEE TEH OTHR BIOMZ. F3 TELLZ U HOW HIGH U IZ.

## HOW KITTEH FINDZ OUT

NO BIOM NAEM ON SCREEN YET, BUT U SEE BIG ROCKZ FROM SUPR FAR. GO UP AN WATCH TEH BLOKZ CHANGE WIF HEIGHT.
