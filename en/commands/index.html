    Commands

    Open the console with T or /; Tab completes, up/down recalls commands.

    /helpLists the commands, or details one.
    /tpTeleports (~5 = 5 blocks further).
    /givePuts an item in the selected slot.
    /gamemodeChanges the game mode.
    /timeSets the time (day, noon, night, midnight or ticks).
    /seedShows the world seed.
    /cinematicPlays a cinematic from the packs.
