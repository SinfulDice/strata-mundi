    Description

    A small iron cage whose golden heart glows without flickering. Sturdier and brighter than a torch, it gives homes a cosy evening feel.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or /give lantern. It isn't found in nature.

    Usage

    Lighting houses, squares and bridges, where a torch would look too humble.

    Behaviour

    Gives off the strongest light, level 15, which drops by one level per block travelled. It isn't solid. It stands on a solid block or hangs under a block from its little chain, depending on the face you aim at. Remove the floor or ceiling holding it and the lantern drops.

    Tips

    A lantern reaches a little further than a torch: handy in the middle of a large room.

    Discovery in game

    It's waiting for you in the Building tab. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
