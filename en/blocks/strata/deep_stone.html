    Description

    Darker and denser than ordinary stone, deep stone tells you that you are seriously far below the surface. It looks a bit grumpy; that's only because it carries the rest of the world on its back.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give deep_stone. In the world, it replaces stone below a certain depth, in every biome. The border between the two isn't perfectly flat: it wobbles by a few blocks. Further down still, you reach bedrock.

    Usage

    Dark-toned building, cellar floors, contrast with pale stone. It's also the rock that hosts the deep variants of the ores.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block. It is harder than stone: that changes nothing in creative, where everything breaks in one click, but it will matter once survival arrives.

    Tips

    Diamond only shows up near the very bottom: when stone turns into deep stone, you're on the right track.

    Discovery in game

    Its name appears at the top of the screen when you aim at it; F3 also shows your altitude, handy to note where the deep layer begins.
