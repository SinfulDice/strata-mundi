    Description

    The cactus, prickly king of the desert. Green, ribbed, bristling with spines, it grows bolt upright and never asks to be watered. The ideal housemate.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give cactus. In the world, it only grows in the desert, on sand, as small columns a few blocks tall, scattered among the dunes.

    Usage

    Desert decoration, dry gardens, very dissuasive fences (in looks, at least).

    Behaviour

    A solid, motionless block. For now, it doesn't prick: damage arrives with survival. It doesn't grow after the world is generated either, and in creative it can be placed on any solid block.

    Tips

    Its columns stick out above the dunes: from afar, a cactus is the best sign that you're nearing the desert.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. The desert is dotted with them from your very first step.
