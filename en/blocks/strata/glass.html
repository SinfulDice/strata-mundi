    Description

    A clear pane with a thin frame, through which you can admire the landscape without catching a cold. Glass is the promise of a bright, airy home.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or /give glass. It never appears in nature: it's a builder's product through and through.

    Usage

    Windows, glass roofs, greenhouses, aquariums, underwater observatories. Anything that needs to see outside (or inside).

    Behaviour

    A solid, transparent block: only its frame is drawn, the rest lets everything show through. You can walk on it without trouble. Two glass blocks side by side don't merge into one big pane yet: each block keeps its own frame.

    Tips

    Combine glass and still water for an underwater corridor: place the panes where the water is, then dig out the inside.

    Discovery in game

    It's waiting for you in the Building tab. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
