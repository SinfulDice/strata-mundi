    Description

    Deep stone, dark and dense, with the same coppery flecks as copper ore. The contrast makes them even easier to spot.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give deep_copper_ore. In the world, it is copper ore wherever stone gives way to deep stone, in small veins as well as at the bottom of giant deposits.

    Usage

    A decorative block for now: ores can't be harvested yet.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block, harder than ordinary copper ore.

    Tips

    Giant deposits that dive below the deep stone line change colour on the way: follow the vein.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen; F3 shows you've gone a long way down.

    Generation

    • In veins between heights 30 and 63.
    • In rare giant deposits, around heights 40 to 120.
