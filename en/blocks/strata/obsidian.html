    Description

    A glassy, purplish-black rock born where water meets lava. Very hard, it gleams softly when the light grazes it.

    Obtaining

    Pour water onto a lava source (or let water flow to it): the lava sets into obsidian. It is also in the creative inventory (Natural tab).

    Usage

    Building and dark decoration. Its great hardness will matter once mining takes time (v0.3).

    Behaviour

    A full solid block, like stone. It neither flows nor melts.

    Tips

    To set a lava lake, let water flow over it: sources turn into obsidian, flowing lava into stone.

    Discovery in game

    You find it by watching water meet lava deep in the caves.
