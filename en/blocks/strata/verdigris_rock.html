    Description

    A grey-green rock, weathered like an old copper roof. It makes up the body of giant copper deposits.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give verdigris_rock. In the world, it is found in giant copper deposits, rare and rich, several dozen blocks long. A few blocks sometimes crop out on the surface, right above.

    Usage

    A building block for now, with a cool, soft tone.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block, a little harder than stone.

    Tips

    Greenish stones in the grass or the sand point to a copper deposit underneath. They are easier to spot from afar, from high up.

    Discovery in game

    Spot it while exploring: its green tint stands out among grey rocks.

    Generation

    • In rare giant deposits, around heights 40 to 120.
    • Here and there on the ground, above a giant deposit.
