    Description

    Stone gleaming with little bright-yellow nuggets: gold, gold! The kind of block that makes you squeal with joy around a cave bend.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give gold_ore. In the world, gold hides in the lower half of the world, in veins smaller and rarer than iron's. Since its heights mostly dip into deep stone, you'll meet it far more often in its deep form; in stone, it only shows up in a thin band just above the deep layer.

    Usage

    A decorative block for now, and what a decoration! Ores can't be harvested yet: breaking it in creative makes it vanish.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as stone.

    Tips

    To find some, follow caves that head down towards the border between stone and deep stone.

    Discovery in game

    Its nuggets catch the eye from afar in a cave. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.

    Generation

    • In veins between heights 64 and 70.
