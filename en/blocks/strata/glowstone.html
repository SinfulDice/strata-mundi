    Description

    A golden stone full of glowing grains, as warm to the eye as a fireplace.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Building tab of the inventory, or /give glowstone. It isn't found in nature yet.

    Usage

    Glowing ceilings, lit floors, lighthouses: anywhere the light should be part of the wall.

    Behaviour

    A full, solid block giving off light of level 15. Like stone, it stops light coming from elsewhere, but shines its own all around.

    Tips

    Set into a ceiling or a floor, it gives light with nothing sticking out.

    Discovery in game

    It's waiting for you in the Building tab. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
