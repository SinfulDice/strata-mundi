    Description

    A piece of a giant brown mushroom cap, wide and flat like a table.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give brown_mushroom_block. In the world, it forms the flat cap of giant brown mushrooms, in the giant mushroom lands.

    Usage

    Platforms, flat roofs, giant picnic tables.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, soft block: it breaks quickly.

    Tips

    The top of a brown mushroom is a great lookout over the surroundings.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at a giant mushroom cap to read its name at the top of the screen.

    Generation

    • In the vegetation: Mushroom Fields
