    Description

    The deep version of coal ore: black spots set into deep stone, dark on dark. You need a sharp eye to spot it, but it's definitely there.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give deep_coal_ore. In the world, it appears instead of coal ore wherever a vein runs through deep stone, so in the depths of every biome.

    Usage

    A decorative block for now. Like every ore, it can't be harvested yet: in creative, breaking it makes it vanish without leaving anything behind.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as deep stone, and harder than its surface cousin (which will matter in survival).

    Tips

    A vein can straddle the border between stone and deep stone: you'll then see both variants side by side, handy to spot where the deep layer begins.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen: the word "Deep" tells you you've crossed into deep stone.

    Generation

    • In veins between heights 5 and 63.
