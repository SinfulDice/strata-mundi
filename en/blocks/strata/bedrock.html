    Description

    Bedrock is the floor of the world. A rough, dark, stubborn layer laid at the very bottom so that nothing slips out underneath.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give bedrock. In the world, it lines the bottom of every column: one continuous row at the very bottom, topped with a few scattered blocks that make its surface bumpy. Caves never carve into it.

    Usage

    In creative, it's a decorative block with plenty of character. In survival, it will be a hard limit: whatever lies beneath stays out of reach.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block. It is unbreakable outside creative mode. In creative, your all-powerful click gets through it like anything else, but break it sparingly: it's there for a reason.

    Tips

    If patches of bedrock show up while you dig, you've hit rock bottom: climb back a little, that's where diamond-bearing rock hides.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen. F3 shows your altitude: when that number gets tiny, bedrock is never far.
