    Description

    Grey stone speckled with matte black spots: coal. It's the most common ore in the world, the one you bump into around every cave corner.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give coal_ore. In the world, it forms small veins in stone, over a very wide range of heights: from the depths right up into the mountainsides, where it sometimes shows in the open air. In deep stone, it takes the form of deep coal ore.

    Usage

    For now, it's a decorative block that looks nice in a stone wall. Ores can't be harvested yet: breaking one in creative simply makes it vanish. It will become a real resource with survival.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block, from the same family as stone.

    Tips

    Coal is the ore that climbs the highest: if you see black dots on a mountain cliff, it's nearly always coal.

    Discovery in game

    Explore a cave: you can spot it from afar. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen; F3 gives you the vein's altitude.

    Generation

    • In veins between heights 64 and 250.
