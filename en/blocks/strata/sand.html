    Description

    Golden sand, fine and warm, the kind that makes you want to kick off your shoes. You find it where it's hot and where there's water, which is convenient for a holiday.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give sand. In the world, it covers the whole desert (with sandstone underneath) and the land rising out of the ocean. It also forms the beaches and the shallow bed along plains and desert shores.

    Usage

    Beaches, zen gardens, giant sandcastles. It's also the only ground cacti grow on.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque block. For now, sand doesn't fall: gravity hasn't reached the world yet, so your sand arches stand up on their own. Enjoy it while it lasts.

    Tips

    Sand is the best hint that water is close: in the plains, a strip of sand often means a beach just beyond.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen; type "sand" in the creative inventory search to find it instantly.

    Generation

    • Below the surface: Ocean
    • On the surface: Desert, Ocean
    • Under water: Desert, Plains
