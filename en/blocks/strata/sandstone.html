    Description

    Sandstone is sand that took its time to settle down. Its sides show pretty horizontal layers, and its top is as smooth as a temple floor tile.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give sandstone. In the world, it forms a layer a few blocks thick right under the desert sand, before the stone.

    Usage

    Desert architecture, pyramids, colonnades, bathhouses. It goes beautifully with sand and fir planks.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block. The top and bottom faces differ from the sides, giving striped walls and plain floors. Unlike sand, it will never need support: this block holds itself together.

    Tips

    Dig a few blocks under the dunes to find a ready-made stash of sandstone, no need to look any further.

    Discovery in game

    You'll come across it while digging in the desert. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or middle-click to grab it.
