    Description

    Bright flames dancing above the ground. Fire gives as much light as glowstone, warms the mood… and devours wood.

    How to get it

    It has no item: light it with flint and steel (Tools tab of the creative inventory), right clicking a face.

    Behaviour

    Gives light of level 15. It is not solid: you walk through it, and placing a block in its place removes it. It stands on a solid block or next to a flammable one, never next to water. It ages: with nothing to burn around, it soon dies out; otherwise it spreads to the logs, planks, leaves, grass and flowers nearby, which end up consumed. The "Fire" mechanics page explains it all.

    Tips

    A water bucket poured next to it puts it out at once. For a campfire that destroys nothing, use the world option /fire destroy off.

    Discovering it in game

    Light a flame on stone: it dies on its own. Light it against a trunk: the forest will remember.
