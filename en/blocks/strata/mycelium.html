    Description

    Dirt covered in a mauve felt of mushroom threads. Soft underfoot, a little mysterious.

    Obtaining

    In creative: Nature tab of the inventory, or /give mycelium. In the world, it covers the rare mushroom fields.

    Usage

    Mushroom gardens, fairy floors, mushroom-house scenery.

    Behaviour

    Full, opaque block; the underside is dirt.

    Tips

    Giant and small mushrooms happily grow on it.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
