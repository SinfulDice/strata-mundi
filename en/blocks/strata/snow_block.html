    Description

    A block of tightly packed snow, dazzling white with bluish glints. It crunches just from looking at it.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give snow_block. In the world, it caps the highest mountain peaks, above the band of bare stone. Careful: the snowy tundra is covered with snowy grass blocks, not snow blocks.

    Usage

    Monumental snowmen, igloos, high-altitude chalets, or simply artificial peaks for mountains of your own.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block. For now it never melts, not even placed in the middle of the desert: this is snow of rare determination.

    Tips

    Fly up to the top of a mountain to admire its snowcap: the view over the ridges is worth the trip. F3 tells you how high you've climbed.

    Discovery in game

    Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or search "snow" in the creative inventory: you'll also find its cousin, the snowy grass block.
