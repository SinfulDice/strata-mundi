    Description

    A grass block wearing its woolly hat: a layer of snow on top, grass peeking shyly out of the sides, and dirt underneath. It's cold out there, but it looks perfectly cosy.

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give snowy_grass_block. In the world, it forms the ground of the snowy tundra. Near the frozen water, the shores are made of gravel.

    Usage

    Winter landscapes, mountain villages, very early holiday decorations. The tundra's firs take root on it.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque block, with snow on top, mixed snow-and-grass sides and dirt underneath. For now, the snow never melts and never spreads: it stays as it is wherever you put it, even in the middle of the desert.

    Tips

    Mix it with ordinary grass blocks for a natural snow line instead of a dead-straight border.

    Discovery in game

    You'll recognise it from your first step into the tundra. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen.
