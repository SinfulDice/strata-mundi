    Description

    Glowing orange molten rock sleeping at the bottom of the world. It makes for splendid underground lakes… best admired from a distance.

    Obtaining

    Lava has no item of its own: in creative, the lava bucket (Tools tab) pours a source, and the empty bucket takes it back. In the world, it fills caves, caverns and ravines at the very bottom, near the bedrock, in lakes with a flat surface.

    Usage

    Scenery of the depths and a landmark: when you see it, the bedrock is not far below.

    Behaviour

    Lava flows, but slowly (one block every 1.5 seconds) and not far: 3 blocks on flat ground, downwards first. The world's lava stays still until disturbed. Touching water, a lava source turns into obsidian and flowing lava into stone. It isn't solid: you sink into it and swim very slowly, much harder than in water; with your head inside, all you see is an orange fog. It doesn't burn yet, but it gives off light (level 15) all around. Only an empty bucket can target it and it can't be broken; however, you can place a block in its place.

    Tips

    To cross a lava lake, place blocks on its surface and walk on them.

    Discovery in game

    Since it can't be targeted, its name never appears at the top of the screen: you recognise it on sight when you go all the way down into the caves.
