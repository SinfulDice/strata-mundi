    Description

    Good old dirt: brown, crumbly, comforting. It hides under the grass and smells lovely after the rain (well, it will, once it rains).

    Obtaining

    In creative: the Natural tab of the inventory, or /give dirt. In the world, it forms a layer a few blocks thick under the surface of plains, forests, mountains and snowy tundra, between the grass and the stone. It's also the bottom face of grass blocks.

    Usage

    Earthworks, artificial hills, first makeshift huts. It goes down anywhere and comes off just as fast.

    Behaviour

    A full, opaque, motionless block. For now, dirt stays dirt: grass doesn't grow back on it, even in full sunshine. Patience.

    Tips

    Need quick scaffolding? A pillar of dirt goes up and comes down in a blink in creative.

    Discovery in game

    Break a grass block and it appears underneath. Aim at it to read its name at the top of the screen, or search "dirt" in the creative inventory.

    Generation

    • Below the surface: Forest, Mountains, Plains, Snowy Tundra
