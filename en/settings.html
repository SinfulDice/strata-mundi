    Settings

    Settings for every machine

    Strata Mundi's golden rule: everything can be turned on, off or adjusted. Whether you play on a powerful PC, an old laptop or the DS handheld console, you can tailor the game to your machine, your eyes and your habits. Each option comes with a short sentence explaining what it does, performance included.

    Presets

    A preset sets all the graphics at once: Very low, Low, Medium, High, Ultra, and DS, designed for the small home-made handheld console. Touching a single graphics setting afterwards switches to Custom, without losing anything else.

    Applied right away

    Changes apply live, without restarting: the game only redoes what changed (re-meshing the world, textures, window…). Settings are sorted into six tabs (Graphics, Interface, Controls, Keys, Game, Packs) and saved in a single file; an invalid value simply goes back to its default.

    Accessibility

    A readable font, interface size, colour-blind filters, reduced camera effects and an interface layout editor are all in the Interface tab.

    All settings

    The table below details every option and its effect.

    Graphics

    PresetSets all graphics at once.
    Render distanceFarther = more memory and computing.
    Vertical distanceSections drawn above/below you.
    Render scale3D resolution. Lower eases the graphics card.
    Frame rate limitFewer frames = less heat and battery.
    Vertical syncPrevents tearing; may add delay.
    Field of viewViewing angle. No effect on performance.
    Ambient occlusionShadows in corners. Low cost.
    FogFades the distance into the sky. No cost.
    MipmapsCalmer distant textures. A bit of memory.
    Anisotropic filteringSharp distant floors, softened pixels. Medium cost.
    Meshing threadsMore = world shown faster, more CPU.
    Generation threadsMore = world created faster (next world).

    Interface

    LanguageLanguage of the game's texts.
    Interface sizeEnlarges menus and HUD (auto: from the screen).
    Font"Readable": a plain font, easier to read.
    Colour-blind filterMakes confused colours distinguishable.
    Reduce camera effectsNo sprint zoom or view bobbing.
    Pause in every menuOtherwise only the pause menu stops the world.
    Targeted block nameShows the name of the targeted block at the top.
    Arrange the HUD…Move the crosshair, hotbar and block name.

    Controls

    Mouse sensitivityTurning speed with the mouse.
    Stick sensitivityTurning speed with the right stick.
    Invert vertical axisUp and down swapped when looking.
    Dead zoneIgnores small movements of worn sticks.
    Auto jumpJumps up one-block steps by itself.
    Step up half blocksClimbs slabs and stairs without jumping.

    AutosaveMore often = less loss, more writing.
