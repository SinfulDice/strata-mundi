    Settings

    Settings for every machine

    Strata Mundi's golden rule: everything can be turned on, off or adjusted. Whether you play on a powerful PC, an old laptop or the DS handheld console, you can tailor the game to your machine, your eyes and your habits. Each option comes with a short sentence explaining what it does, performance included.

    Presets

    A preset sets all the graphics at once: Very low, Low, Medium, High, Ultra, and DS, designed for the small home-made handheld console. Touching a single graphics setting afterwards switches to Custom, without losing anything else.

    Applied right away

    Changes apply live, without restarting: the game only redoes what changed (re-meshing the world, textures, window…). Settings are sorted into six tabs (Graphics, Interface, Controls, Keys, Game, Packs) and saved in a single file; an invalid value simply goes back to its default.

    Atmosphere

    Two graphics settings dress up the world: Biome blend (fades grass, leaf and water colours across borders, in blocks; off: sharp borders) and Particles (off, reduced or full: fragments of broken blocks, drips under ceilings, bubbles). Blending rebuilds the meshes; particles set to off cost nothing.

    Accessibility

    A readable font, interface size, colour-blind filters, reduced camera effects and an interface layout editor are all in the Interface tab.

    All settings

    The table below details every option and its effect.

    Graphics

    PresetSets all graphics at once.
    Render distanceFarther = more memory and computing.
    Vertical distanceSections drawn above/below you.
    Render scale3D resolution. Lower eases the graphics card.
    Frame rate limitFewer frames = less heat and battery.
    Vertical syncPrevents tearing; may add delay.
    Field of viewViewing angle. No effect on performance.
    Ambient occlusionShadows in corners. Low cost.
    LightingFull: dark caves, torches (one background thread). Flat: everything lit, no cost.
    Smooth lightingBlends light from block to block instead of hard steps. Low cost (meshing).
    BrightnessLifts dark places (caves, night). No cost.
    FogFades the distance into the sky. No cost.
    Animated texturesMoving water, lava, fire and portals, liquid ripples. Almost no cost.
    Texture variantsRandom variants (grass, stone…) and connected textures (glass, bookshelves). Variants cost nothing; connected glass draws a few more faces.
    Biome blendBlends grass, leaf and water colours across biome borders, in blocks (off: sharp borders). A few more faces near borders; changing it rebuilds the meshes.
    StarsTwinkling stars in the night sky. Almost no cost.
    CloudsDrifting pixel-art clouds. Simple: one flat layer, very light. Full: with volume, a little more work for the graphics card.
    MipmapsCalmer distant textures. A bit of memory.
    Anisotropic filteringSharp distant floors, softened pixels. Medium cost.
    VegetationGrass and flowers drawn. Reduced or off eases the graphics card.
    ParticlesFragments of broken blocks, drips under ceilings, bubbles. Reduced: fewer of them; off: nothing computed or drawn.
    Map previewMap of the world in the generation editor, drawn in the background. Coarse: four times fewer points.
    Meshing threadsMore = world shown faster, more CPU.
    Generation threadsMore = world created faster (next world).

    Sound

    Master volumeVolume of every sound. No effect on performance; without a sound card the game runs silently.
    BlocksFootsteps, broken and placed blocks, water, lava and fire.
    AmbiencesBackground ambiences of biomes and caves: wind and gusts, leaves, waves, insects, drips. Block sounds (lava, fire, water) follow "Blocks".
    MusicVolume of the music.
    Music frequencySilence between two pieces: rare (10 to 20 minutes), normal (5 to 10), often (2 to 5). To turn the music off, set its volume to 0.
    SubtitlesWrites every sound heard at the bottom right, with an arrow towards it.
    Spatial audioAdvanced: echo of the place (cave, house, open air) and sounds muffled behind walls. Simple stereo: distance and left/right only, less work (DS preset).

    Interface

    LanguageLanguage of the game's texts.
    Interface sizeEnlarges menus and HUD (auto: from the screen).
    Font"Readable": a plain font, easier to read.
    Colour-blind filterMakes confused colours distinguishable.
    Reduce camera effectsNo sprint zoom or view bobbing.
    Pause in every menuOtherwise only the pause menu stops the world.
    Targeted block nameShows the name of the targeted block at the top.
    Arrange the HUD…Move the crosshair, hotbar and block name.
