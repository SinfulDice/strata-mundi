    Description

    A strange and rare corner of the world, with mauve mycelium ground where giant red and brown mushrooms grow. You feel tiny there, like a bug in a garden.

    Obtaining

    Mushroom fields are rare: they only appear where the land is both very humid and very flat, often near swamps.

    Usage

    Mycelium, giant mushroom blocks and ready-made architecture for a mushroom house.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: mycelium on dirt, dirt under the water.
    • Vegetation: giant red and brown mushrooms, lots of small mushrooms.

    Tips

    If you find one, note its position (F3): you won't come across them often.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet: the mauve ground and giant mushrooms are enough to recognise it.
