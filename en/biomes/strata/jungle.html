    Description

    A thick, hot jungle where giant trees with trunks two blocks wide tower over an undergrowth of bushes, ferns and vines.

    Obtaining

    Jungles appear in hot, humid regions, inland.

    Usage

    Jungle wood galore (a single giant tree fills a chest), vines, and a vertical playground for tree houses.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: grass on dirt, gravel under the water.
    • Vegetation: giant jungle trees with vines, oaks, jungle bushes, ferns, tall grass, a few flowers.
    • The undergrowth is dense: you move slower than on the plains.

    Tips

    Climb to the top of a giant tree to scout the area: the canopy can be seen from very far away.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet: the giant trees and packed bushes are enough to recognise it.
