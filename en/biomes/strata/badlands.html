    Description

    Hot, arid terraced plateaus of red sand, where every slope reveals strata of coloured terracotta: orange, yellow, white, red. A postcard landscape.

    Obtaining

    Badlands appear on the plateaus of hot, dry regions, high above the sea.

    Usage

    Red sand and terracotta in five colours, already sorted by nature for your builds.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: red sand on flat tops, terracotta strata on slopes and under the surface (about thirty blocks deep), gently waving.
    • Vegetation: dead bushes, rare cacti.

    Tips

    To gather a given colour, follow its layer along a slope: it stays at the same height.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet: the coloured bands are enough to recognise the badlands.
