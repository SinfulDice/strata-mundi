    Description

    Wide stretches of soft grass rolling as far as the eye can see, dotted with lone oaks. The plains are the biome of picnics, long strolls and first houses.

    Obtaining

    Plains appear in temperate, rather dry regions, on gentle terrain. They're one of the two biomes where the world can make you start (along with the forest).

    Usage

    Ideal building land: flat, open, with just enough trees for decoration.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: grass blocks, with a layer of dirt underneath, then stone.
    • Vegetation: a few scattered oaks.
    • Shores: sand, on the beaches as well as on the shallow bed.
    • Borders with neighbouring biomes are gentle: surface blocks blend a little there.

    Tips

    Start your first base here: the view stretches far, and water is never far from the starting point.

    Discovery in game

    The game doesn't show biome names yet: you recognise plains by their short grass and solitary oaks. F3 gives your position, handy to note where your favourite plains are.
