    Description

    A vast frozen plain carpeted in snow, where a few shivering firs stand and lakes freeze into mirrors of ice. Cold, silent and pretty as a postcard.

    Obtaining

    The snowy tundra appears in the coldest regions, on gentle terrain.

    Usage

    Chalets, igloos, winter villages, and large frozen lakes you can walk across.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: snowy grass blocks, with dirt underneath, then stone.
    • Water: its surface is frozen into a layer of ice, with liquid water right underneath.
    • Vegetation: a few scattered firs.
    • Shores: gravel along the water.
    • For now, snow doesn't melt and it doesn't snow: the landscape stays frozen exactly as it was generated.

    Tips

    Frozen lakes make natural roads: walk across them to save time, the ice is solid.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet: snow on the ground and ice on the water are enough to recognise it. Aim at the ground to read "Snowy Grass Block" at the top of the screen.
