    Description

    A sea of golden sand under a vast sky, where only cacti dare to grow. The desert is hot, dry and surprisingly peaceful. Don't forget your hat.

    Obtaining

    The desert appears in hot, dry regions, on rather gentle terrain.

    Usage

    Sandstone galore right under the surface, wide open spaces for monumental builds, and cacti for decoration.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: sand on the surface, then a layer of sandstone, then stone.
    • Vegetation: cacti, which only grow on sand.
    • Shores: sand, on the beaches as well as underwater.
    • No trees, no grass: the contrast with neighbouring biomes shows from very far away.

    Tips

    The desert is perfect for trying out builds: the land is open and sand is easy to level. And since it doesn't fall yet, your arches stand up on their own.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet, but you can't miss it: endless sand and cacti speak for themselves. Dig a little to discover sandstone.
