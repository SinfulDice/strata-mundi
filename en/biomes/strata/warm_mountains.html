    Description

    The mountains of the hot regions: grassy slopes dotted with acacias, then red sand and bare rock towards the summits. They bridge the temperate mountains and the badlands.

    Obtaining

    Warm mountains rise inland, where the terrain is young and barely eroded, in hot and rather dry regions.

    Usage

    Acacia wood up high, red sand and stone within reach of your pickaxe, and fine views over the neighbouring badlands.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: grass on the lower slopes, with dirt underneath.
    • Altitude: around 165 blocks, grass gives way to red sand, then around 200 blocks to bare stone, all the way to the top: no snow in the hot regions.
    • Vegetation: acacias, tall grass and bushes on the grass, dead bushes on the red sand.
    • Shores: gravel along the water.

    Tips

    Red sand marks the tree line: above it, there are no more acacias to cut.
