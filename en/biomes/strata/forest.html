    Description

    A dense, lush oak forest, where the crowns almost touch and you weave your way between trunks. It smells of moss, bark and adventure.

    Obtaining

    Forests appear in temperate, humid regions, on gentle terrain. Along with the plains, it's one of the two biomes where the world can make you start.

    Usage

    An endless supply of oak logs and leaves, and the perfect setting for a tree house or an elven village… of your own invention.

    Behaviour

    • Ground: grass blocks, with dirt underneath, then stone.
    • Vegetation: lots of oaks, packed close together.
    • Shores: gravel on the banks and on the shallow bed.
    • The transition to neighbouring plains is gentle: the trees gradually thin out.

    Tips

    Fly above the canopy to find your bearings: seen from above, the forest forms a green carpet that's easy to tell apart from the plains.

    Discovery in game

    The biome name isn't shown yet: the density of oaks is enough to recognise it. Aim at a tree to read the name of its blocks at the top of the screen.
