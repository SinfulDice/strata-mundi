    Controls

    Keyboard, mouse and gamepad

    Strata Mundi plays just as well with keyboard and mouse as with a gamepad, and you can switch at any time. On-screen prompts follow the last device you used: keyboard key or gamepad button.

    Any keyboard layout

    The game reads the position of keys, not the printed letter: on a French AZERTY keyboard, movement naturally lands on Z, Q, S, D. Yet the texts show the letter actually printed on your key.

    The gamepad

    Buttons are named as on a New 3DS (A on the right, B at the bottom, X at the top, Y on the left), as on the DS handheld console. Left stick to move, right stick for the camera, with an adjustable dead zone. Every menu works with the gamepad, with an on-screen keyboard for typing text; only the command console needs a real keyboard.

    Remapping

    Every action can be reassigned in Options → Keys, on keyboard as on gamepad: pick the action, confirm, then press the new key. A key already taken elsewhere is removed there, and a button restores the default keys. Sensitivities, inverted axis and auto jump are in Options → Controls.
