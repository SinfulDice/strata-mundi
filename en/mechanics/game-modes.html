    Game modes

    Principle

    The game mode decides a world's rules. It's chosen at creation, on the "New world" screen, and then shown in the world list. In v0.1, only one mode exists: creative. Survival arrives in v0.3.

    Creative mode

    The no-strings-attached sandbox, to build and explore in complete freedom:

    • every block is available in the creative inventory, with tabs and search;
    • you break blocks instantly, even bedrock;
    • you fly by double-tapping jump;
    • every console command is allowed;
    • all knowledge is known in advance: there's nothing to discover before you can build.

    Survival (coming soon)

    Planned for v0.3. It will bring its own rules: harvesting, tools, block hardness that really matters, unbreakable bedrock. Meanwhile, /gamemode survival politely asks you to be patient.

    Changing mode

    The /gamemode creative command puts the world back into creative; the change is saved with the world.

    Discovery in game

    The "New world" screen shows the chosen mode, the world list recalls each world's mode, and /help gamemode describes the command.
