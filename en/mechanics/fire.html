    Fire

    Principle

    Fire is a block of flames that gives light (level 15), ages and spreads. It stands on a solid block or next to a flammable one. At each step (a little over a second and a half), it gets older, may consume a flammable neighbour (sometimes leaving a flame in its place) and light the air around it, up to four blocks above, when that air touches something that burns. The older it is, the less it spreads; a fire without fuel soon dies, and every blaze ends on its own.

    Flint and steel (creative)

    In the Tools tab of the creative inventory, flint and steel lights a fire with a right click on the aimed face, if a fire can stand there (not next to water). The server checks each use (reach, free space), as for placing a block.

    What burns

    • Logs: hard to light, burn slowly.
    • Planks: a little faster.
    • Leaves: catch easily.
    • Grass, ferns, bushes, flowers: go up in an instant.
    • Vines: burn fast but light little air around them.

    Stone, dirt, sand and mushrooms do not burn. Each block's wiki page says whether it is flammable.

    Putting it out

    • Water puts out at once a fire that touches it, and no fire lights next to it.
    • Breaking the fire or placing a block in its place removes it.
    • Without fuel, it dies on its own within seconds.

    Settings

    World options, chosen at creation or with /fire: spread (fire lights new fires) and destroy (what burns is destroyed). Fires share the liquids' budget of updates per tick (/fluids <budget>).
