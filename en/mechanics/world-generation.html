    World generation

    Principle

    The world isn't drawn by hand: it's calculated from a seed, bit by bit as you move forward. Same seed, same world, down to the block, whatever order you explore in. It's practically endless: only the border chosen at creation stops it.

    Terrain and biomes

    Four invisible maps cover the world: temperature, humidity, continentalness (from open sea to deep inland) and erosion (from flat, worn land to young terrain). The last two give the ground height: deep oceans, gentle hills, ridged mountains. The terrain is fully 3D: cliffs, overhangs and arches where the land is young, terraced plateaus inland, and winding rivers that fade out up high, carving dry valleys. The biome, picked from the climate, only decides the ground cover and vegetation, with gentle transitions between neighbours. Biomes stretch over hundreds of blocks and follow heat bands: a desert never touches snow, there are always plains, forests or a savanna in between. Nineteen biomes exist for now.

    Below the surface

    Under the biome's soil come stone, deep stone, then bedrock. Water fills the dips up to sea level. Winding caves run through the rock without opening under the sea, ores form small veins by depth, rare giant deposits of iron or copper stretch for dozens of blocks (a few coloured stones give them away on the surface), and trees and cacti grow according to the biome.

    World types

    On creation, Type picks the world: normal, flat (stacked layers of blocks), amplified (much taller mountains), single biome (one biome everywhere, your choice) or archipelago (mostly sea, dotted with islands). Customize generation… opens the editor: biome size, sea level, cave density and ore frequency (100%: the normal world); depending on the type, the layers of the flat world (add, remove, move up or down, pick the block and thickness) or the biome. A map on the right shows the result around the centre at once, about 2 km across (Map preview option: detailed, coarse or off).

    The Code tab gives a short text summing up all these settings (S1-…): copy it to share it, paste a friend's to get the same kind of world. A damaged code, or one from a newer version, is refused with a message. The seed stays separate: same code and same seed, same world.

    Starting point

    Plains or forest, on dry land, with water nearby.

    Discovery in game

    Explore! F3 shows position and altitude, /seed the seed. All of this is described in the base pack, and other packs can change it.
