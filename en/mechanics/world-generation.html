    World generation

    Principle

    The world isn't drawn by hand: it's calculated from a seed, bit by bit as you move forward. Same seed, same world, down to the block, whatever order you explore in. It's practically endless: only the border chosen at creation stops it.

    Terrain and biomes

    Four invisible maps cover the world: temperature, humidity, continentalness (from open sea to deep inland) and erosion (from flat, worn land to young terrain). The last two give the ground height: deep oceans, gentle hills, ridged mountains. The biome, picked from the climate, only decides the ground cover and vegetation: transitions stay gentle, with no abrupt cliffs. Six biomes exist for now.

    Below the surface

    Under the biome's soil come stone, deep stone, then bedrock. Water fills the dips up to sea level. Winding caves run through the rock without opening under the sea, ores form small veins by depth, and trees and cacti grow according to the biome.

    Starting point

    Plains or forest, on dry land, with water nearby.

    Discovery in game

    Explore! F3 shows position and altitude, /seed the seed. All of this is described in the base pack, and other packs can change it.
